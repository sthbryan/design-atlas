## Patterns worth reusing

- Combine three filter axes (style, interaction, industry) in a niche gallery to get real depth without needing thousands of entries.
- Treat a 404 as a chance for a small interaction — a game, an animated illustration, a custom cursor — instead of a plain message.
- A "surprise me" random shortcut is a simple, cheap way to boost exploration on a small gallery.
- State a clear copyright/usage policy on the gallery itself to remove ambiguity about how the shown content may be used.

## Pitfalls

- Screenshots are copyrighted to their original owners and shown for inspiration only — never copy or redistribute.
- Being a narrow niche, example volume is smaller than in general galleries; pair it with another source for variety.
- No licence or pricing is typically stated for the site itself.
