# Lowtide high-score night

Lowtide is an arcade bar in Hull. On the last Friday of each month it runs a high-score night on its own cabinets. This leaderboard shows on the 55-inch TV above the bar (seen from across the room) and on phones via a QR code.

## Leaderboard
- Data: `src/data/scores.json`, one entry per run: initials (three letters), game, score.
- Show the top score per player per game, ranked highest first within each game. Ranks are computed, never typed.
- Games tonight: Comet Rally and Brick Tower (the bar's own cabinet names).
- Prize: a free drink token for the top score on each game. Nothing else.

## Facts we cannot publish
Player names beyond initials, and any all-time records (we do not have them).
