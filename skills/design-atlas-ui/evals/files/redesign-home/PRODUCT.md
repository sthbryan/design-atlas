# Copperline

- A monthly subscription that covers servicing and repairs at partner bike shops.
- Audience: commuters in Bristol and Bath who ride daily.
- Plans and prices: not public yet; the waitlist collects email and postcode.
- Brand: copper orange #B4541F on near-black #1A1714; the wordmark is set in the logo file only.
- Real numbers: 14 partner shops at launch.
