# Slab

Slab is an independent quarterly of architecture criticism about post-war concrete buildings in the north of England. Two editors, freelance writers, printed on uncoated stock and published free on the web.

## Audience
Architects, students and local-history readers. Most web readers arrive on a phone from a link to one essay.
