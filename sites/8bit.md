# 8bit.ai

## What it is

8bit.ai presents GPU infrastructure, its Neutrino enterprise AI platform and implementation services. The homepage introduces them through a cinematic scroll sequence rather than a conventional static hero.

## When to open it

Open it when you are designing a high-impact technology landing page and want to see how a dark 3D scene, oversized type and a guided scroll can carry a long enterprise story.

## Most useful

- **Opening scene**: white diagonal light trails cross a nearly black field around a softly glowing 8-shaped mark. A small wordmark stays at the upper left while a large, thin headline settles at the lower left.
- **Scroll progression**: scrolling changes the headline and scene while retaining the same central mark and moving light. The next section introduces accelerated compute and AI services within that persistent frame.
- **Navigation and pacing**: the menu collapses to a small upper-right icon; a persistent scroll prompt, sound control and social links sit at the bottom edge.
- **Service storytelling**: later page sections separate the cloud platform, Neutrino and consulting into clear groups with short descriptions and carousel controls.

## Watch out for

- The opening scene remained on a loading screen for several seconds before its content appeared. Give the 3D and motion assets time to load before assessing the page.
- The hero depends on motion and a dark animated scene. Keep equivalent message and navigation available when animation, sound or WebGL is unavailable.
- Commercial services are described but public prices were not found; do not infer a tier or rate from the marketing copy.
- No reuse licence for the site design, code or imagery was stated. Treat it as a look-only reference.

## Reusable ideas

- Keep one recognizable 3D mark in place while scene lighting and copy evolve through the scroll.
- Let a single directional light motif supply depth and movement across an otherwise restrained palette.
- Break a complicated enterprise offer into distinct chapters, with an explicit scroll cue that keeps visitors oriented.
