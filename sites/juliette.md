# Juliette

## What it is

The portfolio of Juliette, an independent designer working across design, blockchain and AI. It combines a short introduction, selected projects, experiments, writing and contact details in one long page.

## When to open it

Open it for a compact editorial portfolio that gives personality through small details rather than a large hero statement. The top uses a narrow text column with a tiny portrait, a multilingual greeting and understated section labels; project names and dates sit on aligned rows with thin dividers. Scroll below the notes to see the loose collage of photographs, interface fragments and doodles used as a visual counterweight to the spare text layout.

## Most useful

- A tiny portrait and multilingual greeting sit above the narrow introduction, setting a personal tone without filling the viewport.
- Projects, playground experiments and essays are separated into labelled groups, but share one restrained type and spacing system.
- The bottom collage gives the page a second visual register without turning each project row into a card.

## Reusable ideas

- Keep the introduction readable in a narrow measure, then give project names and dates separate alignment anchors.
- Use light rules and quiet section labels to organize a long portfolio without enclosing every item in a card.
- Place an irregular image collage after the structured content to add texture while preserving the calm reading flow above.
