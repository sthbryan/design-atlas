# OKOK Services

## What it is

OKOK Services is an independent creative studio portfolio based in Singapore and Amsterdam. Its homepage sets a short studio introduction beside an oversized reflective OK mark on a black field, then links to projects including Balming Tiger, SHINee, Adidas Originals and Local Remote.

## When to open it

Open it to study how an art-directed studio identity leads into commissioned interactive work, or to follow a specific client project from the portfolio into its own live experience.

## Most useful

- **Studio opening**: a compact introduction sits in the upper-left corner while a huge dark chrome OK form fills most of the remaining screen; narrow blue-green reflections give the lettering depth against the black background.
- **Project route**: the Works list pairs each client name with a short description and a direct View Site link, making it possible to move from studio work to the separate commissioned experience.
- **[Balming Tiger case](https://balmingtiger.com/)**: the link from OKOK's portfolio opens a full-screen illustrated room. The live page uses a mustard entry screen, then a 360-degree hand-drawn interior with MUSIC, VIDEO, TOUR, CONTACT and SHOP controls along its upper edge.
- **Scope of evidence**: the OKOK opening composition and the Balming Tiger destination were visually checked. Other portfolio cards were not opened, so their descriptions remain the studio's own claims rather than verified visual examples.

## Using it with agents

No agent-facing integration was visible during the review. Give an agent the OKOK homepage and ask it to compare the studio's black chrome opening with one linked case, such as Balming Tiger, while keeping observations about each site separate.

## Watch out for

- The Balming Tiger case starts behind a click-to-enter screen and recommends audio. Its opening view can be studied without sound, but the full experience may depend on interaction and audio.
- The studio portfolio is free to view, but no pricing for services or reuse terms were stated on the reviewed site. Treat the design, code and client work as look-only.
- This review verified one linked project visually; do not assume every item in the Works list is currently available or behaves the same way.

## Reusable ideas

- Let one oversized, material-rich wordmark carry a sparse studio homepage while keeping the introduction small and easy to find.
- Make project descriptions and destination links distinct so visitors can move from a studio's presentation to the commissioned work itself.
- Give an interactive case its own strong opening frame and a small persistent navigation for its main content areas.
