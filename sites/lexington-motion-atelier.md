# Lexington Motion — Atelier Reels

## What it is

Atelier is one collection in Lexington Motion's paid library of Remotion video templates. The page groups vertical reel concepts for fashion boutiques into editorial and white collections. It presents the templates themselves as motion examples, separate from the Lexington site's own catalogue and sales interface.

## When to open it

- When exploring product-reel motion for fashion, apparel or other image-led shops.
- When comparing editorial and grid-based treatments for a sequence of looks.
- When looking for a concrete reference for transitions, pacing and composition in a vertical video.

## Most useful

The [Atelier collection](https://lexingtonthemes.com/remotion/atelier-reel-templates) names ten concepts, including Edit, Outfit, Lineup, Sale and Rail in the editorial set, and Grid, Checker, Mosaic, Swap and Scroll in the white set. The page describes the motion idea for each concept, making it possible to choose a direction before opening a project. The same catalogue links to other [reel styles](https://lexingtonthemes.com/remotion), such as Estate, Foundry, Gallery and Greenhouse; these are templates from the library, not independent live shop sites.

## Using it with agents

Lexington offers a separate paid [MCP](https://lexingtonthemes.com/mcp) that supplies design systems and sections from its themes to coding agents. The page advertises Cursor, Claude Code and Codex support. The MCP provides access to Lexington Themes resources; the Atelier template page does not state that the motion templates themselves are available through it. Current bundle and plan details are on the [pricing page](https://lexingtonthemes.com/pricing).

## Watch out for

- The visual catalogue describes templates; confirm the exact rendered result and available props in the product before relying on a concept.
- Commercial and client use depends on the applicable paid license. The [license terms](https://lexingtonthemes.com/legal/license) prohibit redistributing the resources themselves as reusable templates.
- The offer banner and prices can change; check the live pricing page before purchasing.

## Reusable ideas

- Name each motion treatment by its visual action, so a large library is scannable without watching every clip.
- Offer distinct art directions for the same content, such as editorial staging and a brighter grid treatment.
- Describe the sequence in plain language next to its preview so viewers can compare concepts quickly.
