# Kush Bothra

## What it is

Kush Bothra's product design portfolio introduces the designer through a spacious, gridded canvas, then leads into featured case studies, archived projects and experiments.

## When to open it

Open the homepage for an expressive portfolio hero that gives a designer's identity the same visual weight as the featured work. The case study and playground links lead into deeper project and experiment pages.

## Most useful

- A fine measurement grid and small live clock make the page feel like an active design workspace.
- The oversized, rounded name sits at the center like a wordmark, with tilted role and availability labels breaking the grid around it.
- Small project previews float at the edges of the hero, giving the visitor a preview of the work before the featured-work section.
- A compact, icon-led navigation groups home, about, case studies, playground and writing across one row.

## Using it with agents

No published agent channel was found. Give an agent the homepage and ask it to describe how the grid, central type and floating project previews establish hierarchy; use the live site as visual reference only.

## Watch out for

- The scattered labels and previews rely on careful positioning; keep the primary introduction readable when adapting the composition to narrower screens.
- No reuse licence was stated for the portfolio's images, writing or case-study work.

## Reusable ideas

- Place one large identity element on a quiet grid, then use a few angled labels to add movement without filling the background.
- Let small previews enter the hero from its edges to connect the introduction with the work below.
- Use short, icon-led navigation labels when the destinations are familiar and the page has a clear visual identity.
