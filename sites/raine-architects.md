# Raine Architects

## What it is

Raine Architects is an architecture studio portfolio. Its dedicated 3D visualization page places a large residential rendering inside a quiet white frame, with vertical previous and next controls at the sides and the project title below.

## When to open it

Open it to study how architectural renderings can take over most of the viewport while navigation and project context remain compact, or to compare presentation patterns for a small project sequence.

## Most useful

- **Rendering scale**: the Bayview Residence image occupies a broad central panel, showing the house, drive, planting and sky in one landscape view.
- **Editorial frame**: fine border lines and generous white space separate the image from the small brand navigation and blue project title.
- **Sideways browsing cue**: previous and next controls sit vertically on opposite margins, making the sequence feel like a gallery rather than a standard card grid.
- **Project action**: a small “Step Inside” link beneath the title signals that the image can lead into a deeper view.

## Using it with agents

No agent-facing integration was visible during the review. The 3D page's image and navigation were accessible by browser; the “Step Inside” destination and the full interactive behavior were not verified.

## Watch out for

- The 3D page initially showed a progress indicator and became visible only after waiting for it to reach 100%. Allow the scene to finish before judging it empty.
- The homepage carousel displayed multiple “Record not found” messages at review, so its content sequence may be incomplete.
- No reuse licence was found for the imagery, code or design. Treat it as look-only; pricing and terms for architecture services were not stated on the reviewed pages.

## Reusable ideas

- Give a single project image room to communicate its materials and setting before adding supporting copy.
- Keep previous and next controls on the outer edges so they do not compete with the rendering.
- Place one explicit deeper-view action directly below each project title.
