# MotionWiki

## What it is

MotionWiki is a catalog of packaged creative systems for building brands, websites, and campaigns with AI. Entries combine different pieces such as codebases, sequenced prompts, skills, assets, templates, and setup instructions. The free Open Library includes workflow examples and skill previews; paid access adds complete systems and member files ([Market](https://www.motionwiki.com/market), [Pricing](https://www.motionwiki.com/pricing)).

The site is also a visual reference. Its own pages pair large editorial headings and quiet grid backgrounds with dark system panels, while catalog cards describe the outcome and implementation ingredients. Many previews require access, so treat the visible product pages as references to the catalog and its presentation rather than as proof of every packaged result.

## When to open it

Open the [Market](https://www.motionwiki.com/market) when you need a repeatable prompt or implementation workflow, or want to browse systems grouped by product type, role, tool, and access. Open individual entries when you want to inspect a specific build brief, its implementation stack, or the included free prompt. For visual inspiration, study the catalog's own restrained editorial layout and look for open previews before treating a system as an inspectable example.

## Most useful

- The [Market](https://www.motionwiki.com/market) has a text search and filters for product type, role, tool, and access. At review it listed 13 systems, including free skills and prompts alongside gated codebase systems.
- The [KnocX Reconstruction entry](https://www.motionwiki.com/market/knocx-reconstruction) shows the shape of a video-led editorial build: a scroll-controlled visual story, Astro and Tailwind CSS, GSAP/ScrollTrigger, chapter navigation, and a reduced-motion fallback. Its demo first showed a buffering state; after another wait it loaded. This is a concrete example description and demo, not a claim that its source files are freely available.
- The [Brand System Foundation Kit preview](https://www.motionwiki.com/market/brand-system-foundation-kit) describes a prompt-led route from an open-ended brand brief to palette and type direction. Use its outcome and ingredients as inspiration for structuring your own process, not as a ready-made style to copy.

## Using it with agents

The [Market](https://www.motionwiki.com/market) describes systems that include prompts and skills for tools such as Claude and AI coding agents. A free prompt link was present on the [KnocX page](https://www.motionwiki.com/market/knocx-reconstruction), but its raw Markdown route could not be opened in this browser review. I found no public MCP, API, CLI, or `llms.txt` instructions on the reviewed pages, so this entry records no verified agent channel.

## Watch out for

- The [pricing page](https://www.motionwiki.com/pricing) separates free Open Library items from one-time Build Kits, Vault membership, and Studio access. Pricing and feature access differ by item.
- Some system thumbnails and files require access. Read the item's own licence notes before reusing its code or assets; the [terms](https://www.motionwiki.com/terms) reserve ownership and restrict redistribution.
- The KnocX demo needed additional time after initially reporting that it was buffering.

## Reusable ideas

- In the site's own interface, study the oversized, left-aligned title against a fine grid and the contrasting dark panel that explains a four-step system. The compact navigation and uppercase mono labels establish hierarchy without competing with the headline.
- As a research tool, start with the desired outcome, then narrow by role, product type, and stack. Compare a free skill preview with a full system page to see how the catalog communicates what is visible and what access is required.
- For a cinematic editorial implementation, the KnocX entry points to a useful interaction sequence to study: chapters coordinated with a video frame sequence, responsive navigation, and a reduced-motion fallback.
