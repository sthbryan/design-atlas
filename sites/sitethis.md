# SiteThis

## What it is

SiteThis is a curated website archive with a large set of pinned references, weekly picks and category, style and color browsing. Detail pages combine palette swatches and editorial notes with a link to the original site.

## When to open it

Open it when you want a quick visual survey by broad category or style, or when palette colors are useful alongside a live design reference.

## Most useful

- Browse the category, style and color links from the homepage, or open a featured card for its palette, notes and original-site link.
- The [Daylight record](https://sitethis.com/site/daylight) shows five palette colors and groups the site under Hardware & devices, Minimal and Editorial. Its [live site](https://daylightcomputer.com/) uses a dark, full-bleed product photograph, a large serif headline and a bright orange call to action.
- The homepage pairs large image cards with narrow palette strips, making color differences easy to compare while scanning.

## Using it with agents

No MCP, API, `llms.txt` or agent-specific interface was visible on the reviewed pages. Agents can browse the public categories and follow record links to inspect the current source site. The gallery's palette and notes are useful discovery metadata; verify the original before making implementation claims.

## Watch out for

- Featured previews can differ from current live pages. Check the outbound source before relying on a design detail.
- No general reuse licence was stated. Use the gallery and linked sites as visual references, and check the creator's terms before reusing assets or code.

## Reusable ideas

- Pair a site screenshot with a small palette strip and concise editorial tags to make visual browsing more specific.
- Let broad category and style links coexist with hand-picked featured references.
