# Daniel White

## What it is

A personal portfolio arranged like a short illustrated book. Its contents page divides the author's story into seven chapters and links onward to selected projects.

## When to open it

Use it as a reference for a portfolio that should feel authored and literary rather than like a conventional project grid. It is especially useful for editorial typography, quiet pacing and a distinctive introduction to the person behind the work.

## Most useful

The [live home page](https://www.danielwhite.uk) opens with a centered, high-contrast serif title and italic subtitle on a warm, pale background. A framed monochrome engraving sits above spacious chapter navigation; the numbered contents and inset project links make the page read like a book. Follow chapters such as Workshop or Voyage to see how the narrative continues into project material.

## Using it with agents

No MCP, API, CLI, `llms.txt` or agent guide was found in the reviewed site. The portfolio can be opened in a browser as a visual reference.

## Watch out for

This is one personal portfolio rather than a reusable pattern library. Its illustrations and writing have no stated reuse licence; use the page for visual study and create original material.

## Reusable ideas

- Give portfolio navigation a narrative structure and keep the contents visible enough to browse.
- Pair an understated paper-like background with dark serif type and a single framed illustration.
- Use generous vertical spacing and short captions to give editorial content room to breathe.
