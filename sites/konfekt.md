# Konfekt

## What it is

Konfekt is a Helsinki design studio focused on packaging, visual identities and branding. The work index presents recent package and identity projects, with older editorial and brand examples further down the list.

## When to open it

Open it when you need packaging and identity references, especially examples where a quiet portfolio frame lets saturated product work take the lead.

## Most useful

- **Home page**: a large, left-aligned statement sits in a pale, spacious field; a wide strip of project photography begins below it.
- **Karl Fazer – Thins**: [the case](https://www.konfekt.fi/work/karl-fazer-thins) opens with a tightly cropped diagonal arrangement of chocolate wrappers. Repeated script branding and product illustrations sit across strong blue, red, pink, cream and black packs.
- **Work index**: recent entries are grouped by year and project name, while the simple top navigation keeps Work and About in view.
- The About page identifies the studio in Helsinki, Finland, and lists branding, packaging, layout, publication, motion and illustration services. Public prices were not stated.

## Using it with agents

No MCP, API, CLI, `llms.txt` or agent guide was found. Link the desired case directly and describe the packaging hierarchy, crop or palette to study.

## Watch out for

- The portfolio images show client work; no reuse licence was stated on the pages checked.
- The examples are strongest for brand and packaging decisions, rather than reusable web components or implementation guidance.

## Reusable ideas

- Keep the studio introduction spacious and understated, then let product photography supply the strongest color and texture.
- Show packaging in a repeated, angled arrangement to make a family of variants immediately readable.
- Keep the project index short in its labels so the images remain the main browsing surface.
