# Nous Portal

## What it is

The account and product portal for Nous Research services. Its overview page presents models, hosted tools and subscription options in one dark, long-form interface.

## When to open it

Use it as a visual reference for AI service portals that need to explain several connected capabilities without hiding the account actions. It is a narrow product example rather than a gallery.

## Most useful

- The [overview](https://portal.nousresearch.com/) uses a fixed left navigation rail and a large editorial-style serif headline against a deep navy field.
- The same page moves from a concise service introduction to numbered benefit blocks, getting-started choices, a searchable model list, and subscription cards.
- The [plans section](https://portal.nousresearch.com/manage-subscription) is a useful example of comparing credit-based tiers with a free option and explicit monthly amounts.

## Using it with agents

There is no published design-focused agent integration. A browser can inspect the public overview and pricing pages without an account; some service actions require sign-in.

## Watch out for

The subscription and model details change over time. The Terms of Service define the platform interface and graphics as Nous Research materials; the footer's MIT notice should not be read as permission to reuse service artwork or copy. Use the live site for visual study only.

## Reusable ideas

- Keep product areas in a persistent left rail while the main column tells one continuous story.
- Use small numbered labels and compact descriptions to give dense benefits a consistent scan pattern.
- Present a free tier beside paid tiers, with monthly price and included credits visible in the same card.
