# Lapa Ninja

## What it is

Lapa Ninja is an independent landing page gallery that began in 2015 as one designer's reference folder. At review it listed 7,496 curated pages and 15,000+ full-page screenshots. Entries come with video recordings, OG images, typeface notes and older versions of the site. Around the gallery it has a lot of side content: 600+ free UI kits and templates, lists of design books, courses and tools, a template shop and a short list of design agent skills. Landing Love lists it as a partner gallery.

## When to open it

When you want a big, long-running archive you can filter by year, colour or platform, for example to see how SaaS or agency landing pages changed from 2015 to 2026. It is also a quick place to grab a free Figma or Framer UI kit to start from.

## Most useful

- **90+ categories** with counts, from SaaS (691) and AI (208) to bento grid, isometric and retro style at review
- **Filters by year (2015 to 2026), colour and platform** (Webflow, Framer, WordPress, Shopify, Astro)
- **Motion, OG image, typeface and sections views** alongside the main feed
- **Freebies**: about 646 downloads at review, including Figma and Framer landing pages, UI kits, wireframe kits and illustrations
- **Agent skills page**: 13 third-party design skills (Emil Kowalski, Vercel, Anthropic, shadcn and others) with links to their repositories

## Using it with agents

There is no API, MCP or `llms.txt`, and Cloudflare blocks plain HTTP clients, including requests for `robots.txt`. The policy also forbids scraping or bulk downloading. Browse it yourself and give your agent the live site URLs. The skills page is only a list of links; install those skills from their own repositories.

## Watch out for

- Sponsored cards (Webflow, Framer, Mobbin) and ads appear in the feed for free users
- Free accounts get a limited number of boards and saves
- Freebies and templates come from third parties, so check each one's licence
- Pro renews monthly with no refunds for partial periods, except for billing mistakes reported within 14 days

## Reusable ideas

- Filter by year to show how a style or industry changed over time
- Keep a site's previous versions next to its current one
- Put a clear, plain-language screenshot and takedown policy on its own page
