# MUUUUU.ORG

## What it is

MUUUUU.ORG is a Japanese web design gallery and award site. A featured Site of the Day leads the homepage, followed by a dated archive of recent selections. Each record can credit its designer or studio and is tagged with visual traits and site categories. The site also has a simplified-Chinese version.

## When to open it

- When you want an award-style daily shortlist and design-led studio work, with the credited makers close to the reference.
- When the brief calls for a specific visual move: the category index includes typography, grid, layout, monochrome, background motion, distortion, fluid objects, WebGL/3D and transitions.
- When you want to browse Japanese web references with a second language option.

## Most useful

- Begin with the current [Site of the Day](https://muuuuu.org/), then follow its visual-category tags or browse the dated archive. The homepage showed the September 26, 2026 pick at review.
- The featured [bleibtgleich portfolio](https://bleibtgleich.dev/) was inspected at review. Its opening screen uses a nearly white field, a thin vertical rule, an asymmetrical grid and very large black type, with small role and location labels at the edges. This is useful for studying editorial hierarchy and scale without relying on its subject matter.
- Follow the designer and studio credits from an entry when you want more work in the same visual language.

## Using it with agents

No MCP, API, `llms.txt` or agent export was visible on the reviewed pages. An agent can use category links to shortlist records, then open each live site and describe its layout, type, color and motion. Treat a gallery capture as a pointer: the actual linked site is the visual evidence for its current behavior.

## Watch out for

- The category tags and curation are primarily Japanese; retain their specific visual meaning rather than translating them into overly broad labels.
- Daily picks and the archive change over time. The review date and linked site should be checked before relying on a capture.
- No general reuse licence was stated for screenshots or featured websites. Use them as look-only references.

## Reusable ideas

- Put one prominent daily selection ahead of the larger archive to give browsing a clear starting point.
- Tag each record along multiple visual axes, including motion and interaction technique as well as color and subject.
- Credit the contributing designer and studio on the entry, then let users follow those credits to related work.
