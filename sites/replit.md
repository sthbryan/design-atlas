# Replit

## What it is

Replit is a browser-based development platform with AI building features, and its homepage leads with the act of building: the question what will you build, answered by the prompt box itself. Creation, not a product mockup, is the opening move.

## When to open it

- When an AI builder's landing page should start at the prompt, not at a feature grid or a screenshot.
- When the target audience is people who create rather than people who deploy, and the page must speak to them first.
- When the product is a platform with many entry points, and the homepage needs one clear opening interaction.

## Most useful

- The prompt box as the hero CTA: the core interaction is the first thing on the page, not a button behind a demo.
- Examples and templates presented as starting points, so visitors arrive with an idea already formed.
- The page structure follows the creative arc (idea, build, share) instead of the product's internal sections.
- The site's visual energy matches the audience: makers, community and momentum over enterprise restraint.

## Using it with agents

- No agent channel is declared on the marketing site at review; the platform itself is the agent surface, behind an account.
- The public pages are the agent reference; building and running projects needs an account.

## Watch out for

- Plan limits and AI usage terms at review are stated on the pricing page; confirm live before budgeting, especially for shared projects.
- Community examples on the site are other users' work; do not reuse them as your own.
- All content is proprietary and look-only; study the prompt-first structure, do not copy the layout or copy.

## Reusable ideas

- Lead an AI builder's homepage with the prompt itself, so the first interaction is the core workflow.
- Present templates and examples as ideas to one-click, not as a gallery to admire.
- Structure the page along the user's creative arc (idea, build, share) rather than the product's module list.
