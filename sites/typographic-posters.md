# typo/graphic posters

## What it is

typo/graphic posters is an international archive of designed, printed posters. Each entry can record its designer, year, country, dimensions and printing process. The archive displayed 12,303 posters from 45 countries at review. Its focus is type and graphic form used as a poster, rather than general artwork or social-media graphics.

## When to open it

Open it for print-minded typography, expressive lettering, poster composition, limited palettes and examples where the printing method is part of the visual idea. It is also useful when you need a reference for large-format type or a sequence of related posters.

## Most useful

- **[Archive](https://www.typographicposters.com/archive)**: filter by country, print process, format, year, animation, awards, series, mood, composition, graphic elements, interactions, iconography and script system. Search is reserved for members.
- **[“La Nouvelle Vague Saison 26-27” by Toan Vu-Huu](https://www.typographicposters.com/toan-vu-huu/6ab54e2a5a84b36764b21476)**: inspect how a pixel grid turns freehand marks into blocky forms, then pair fluid display type with a pixelated face. The page identifies it as a real 2026–27 music-venue season identity and gives the silkscreen size and printer.
- **[Membership details](https://www.typographicposters.com/join)**: at review, paid search covered more than 12,000 posters and included filters for colour, print method, format and series.

## Using it with agents

No documented agent channel appeared on the reviewed pages. Use the archive as a human visual reference: share the stable poster URL, then describe the typographic structure, colour relationships or print treatment you want to explore. Each poster is still its creator's work.

## Watch out for

- Poster images and design rights remain with their respective creators; membership unlocks search, not a licence to reuse the work.
- Some archive controls, including full-text search, require membership. The public archive still supports visual browsing and its visible filters.
- These are print artifacts. Their proportions, density and physical production details may need translating before they fit a responsive screen.

## Reusable ideas

- Let visitors filter a visual archive by formal qualities such as composition, palette, substrate and print process, not only by subject.
- Record the physical dimensions and production method beside an image so its constraints are part of the reference.
- Show related works from the same designer or series to reveal how one visual system varies across pieces.
