# Supahero

- **URL:** https://supahero.io
- **Type:** inspiration gallery
- **Topics:** inspiration, landing-pages, cta
- **Pricing / licence:** Free to browse; paid placements from $79 to $279 per 30 days for featured listings. No reuse licence is stated ("all rights reserved" footer); the heroes belong to the sites shown
- **Reviewed:** 2026-09-25

## What it is

Supahero is a gallery dedicated to website hero sections: the first screen a visitor sees, with its headline, supporting line, visual and call-to-action. A banner says it is now part of ScreensDesign, an app design library, which it promotes throughout the site.

## When to open it

- When you're designing the above-the-fold area of a landing page and want to compare many real approaches side by side.
- When you need to decide between a product-screenshot hero, a typographic hero, a 3D or illustrated hero, or a video hero.
- When you want quick hero references from well-known product sites (Linear, Stripe, Raycast, Attio, Resend and others appear in the list).

## Most useful

- About 570 hero screenshots on a single scrolling page, mixing SaaS products, agencies, portfolios, studios and consumer brands.
- A detail page per hero with the site's tagline, the date it was added and a "Visit Website" link.
- A search box to jump to a brand by name.
- A separate list of around 20 Framer templates for when you want a starting point rather than a reference.
- A submission form for suggesting new hero sections.

## Using it with agents

No MCP, llms.txt, API or export. Pick two or three heroes that match your product, then describe them to the agent: headline length, where the visual sits, number of CTAs, background treatment and what shows before the fold. Ask it to build the structure with your own copy and branding.

## Watch out for

- No filters or tags: browsing is a long scroll or a name search, so you can't narrow by style, industry or layout.
- Sampled entries were dated between October 2024 and December 2025, so newer trends may be missing.
- The Tools page currently shows the same hero list rather than a separate tool directory, and ScreensDesign promotion appears on every page.

## Reusable ideas

- Isolating a single section of the page (the hero) makes comparison fast, because every screenshot answers the same question.
- Reading heroes as a set shows the recurring building blocks: one headline, one supporting line, one primary CTA and one strong visual.
- A simple tiered sponsorship table (badge, premium spot, sitewide feature) with stated impression ranges is an easy model for monetizing a niche gallery.
