# Samwrks

## What it is

Samwrks is Sameer Singh's portfolio for independent web and product design work. Its homepage pairs a persistent profile and contact column with a larger work area that switches between samples and case studies.

## When to open it

Open it for a compact portfolio structure that keeps the designer's availability, short positioning statement and contact actions visible while the work changes. The current work-sample panel uses a warm off-white project frame, a broad photographic hero and a large centered headline; use the live page to inspect the complete project preview.

## Most useful

- The fixed split makes identity and contact information persistent without competing with the featured work.
- Separate Work samples and Case studies tabs let visitors choose between visual scanning and deeper project context.
- A small availability status sits near the profile rather than inside the main promotional copy.

## Using it with agents

No published agent channel was found. Share the live page and describe the split layout or portfolio behavior to study; the case-study work remains the designer's own material.

## Watch out for

- It is a personal portfolio, not a general template library or broad survey of current sites.
- The profile uses external booking and messaging links; verify the destination before copying a contact flow.
- No reuse licence for the visuals or case-study content was stated.

## Reusable ideas

- Keep a concise profile and availability indicator visible beside a browsing portfolio.
- Offer distinct work-sample and case-study views for different levels of visitor intent.
- Frame project previews consistently while letting each project supply its own visual identity.
