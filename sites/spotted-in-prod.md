# Spotted in Prod

## What it is

Spotted in Prod is a gallery of the best iOS app design in production: video clips and stills of real shipped apps, with popular and recent leaderboards, staff picks, home setups and a jobs board.

## When to open it

Open it when you need to see how a shipped iOS app handles a specific interaction, in motion, rather than a static screenshot.

## Most useful

- **Clips, not only stills**: video shows the interaction, which is the point for mobile UI.
- **Leaderboards**: popular and recent rankings surface what the community actually rates.
- **Home setups**: real device setups as a source of layout and widget ideas.

## Using it with agents

No MCP, API or agent guide is published; an agent can browse the clips in a browser.

## Watch out for

- The clips are other apps' work; use them as references, not assets.
- iOS-only coverage, so Android patterns are out of scope.

## Reusable ideas

- Show motion for interaction references; a screenshot cannot carry a transition.
- Use leaderboards to keep a fast-growing gallery navigable.
