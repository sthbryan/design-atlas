# Poetic

## What it is

Poetic sells enterprise workflow automation that learns from procedures and operator feedback. Its public site explains the product through a large typographic landing page and a separate product walkthrough.

## When to open it

When an enterprise AI page needs to make a complex workflow product legible without leading with a feature grid, or when showing system recovery is part of the product story.

## Most useful

- [Home](https://poetic.com/) — inspect the oversized headline, proof metrics, customer-story selector, security strip and careers section.
- [Product](https://poetic.com/product) — inspect the simulated source materials, dispute case interface, feedback step and renamed-tab recovery example.

## Using it with agents

The public pages are available in a browser. I did not find an `llms.txt`, public API, CLI, MCP server or installable agent skill during this review.

## Watch out for

- The examples and performance figures are marketing claims; treat them as content, not independently verified benchmarks.
- The service agreement dates to 2023 and says fees are set in service orders. There is no public price list.
- The terms reserve ownership of the service and software; do not reuse its interface assets or copy.

## Reusable ideas

- Give a multi-step product process a visual walkthrough with realistic source documents, a work surface and a visible recovery case.
- Let proof metrics and customer stories break up a very large headline instead of repeating the same feature-card pattern.
- Pair a strong typographic first screen with more detailed product evidence lower on the page.
