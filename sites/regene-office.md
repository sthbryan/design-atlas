# Regene Office — NOSIGNER

## What it is

NOSIGNER's case study for Regene Office in Yokohama, Japan. It describes an office interior made largely from construction waste salvaged during the demolition of the studio's former office.

## When to open it

Open the [project page](https://nosigner.com/projects/office/) when presenting an architectural or sustainability project through a photographic, editorial web case study.

## Most useful

- The page opens with a darkened, full-width photograph of the office; the project name sits low on the left, while a concise award note rests near the lower edge.
- Sparse top navigation stays over the image, and the page alternates large section labels such as HOW, WHY and WILL with explanatory text and supporting visuals.
- The case explains specific material choices, including repurposed steel frames arranged as ceiling louvers and recycled industrial foil and tiles. The materials are the narrative, not just a backdrop.
- Its information block gives the location as Yokohama and separates project scope from credits. NOSIGNER's [home](https://nosigner.com/) uses the office image as a featured project, linking the portfolio view to the detailed case.

## Using it with agents

No public MCP, API, CLI or installable skill surfaced in the reviewed pages. The home and case are readable in a browser. The site's footer states that NOSIGNER's content is copyrighted; the standard `llms.txt` request was blocked by the browser, so its availability could not be confirmed.

## Watch out for

- The full-screen photograph dominates the first view. Keep a project title and summary legible when using a similar image-first opening.
- The case is about the studio's Yokohama office; its address should not be generalized to every NOSIGNER project.
- Photography and case text are copyrighted, with no reuse permission stated. Use the page for visual study only.

## Reusable ideas

- Lead with a project photograph, then keep the title and a one-line result anchored at the lower edge.
- Give a long case a small set of recurring section labels so the narrative is easy to scan.
- Connect material choices to the design decision they enabled, such as reusing steel frames to form both louvers and service concealment.
