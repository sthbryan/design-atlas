# SceneAI

## What it is

SceneAI is a browsable collection of web-section prompts, landing-page designs, animated backgrounds and gradients. The home page separates landing-page cards from a second grid of animated backgrounds, with preview, prompt-copy and download controls where available.

## When to open it

Use it to study a specific hero direction or to find a moving background treatment for a landing page. The landing-page gallery mixes full-page examples with individual hero sections, so choose the scope that matches the part being designed.

## Most useful

- The [landing-page gallery](https://sceneai.art/landing-pages) has Trending and Recent views and example cards such as Stories in Light, Royal Hero Section and Mundfish Hero Section.
- The [background gallery](https://sceneai.art/backgrounds) offers named, previewable treatments such as Core, Brightly, Nature and Futuristic Cityscape.
- Separate gradient and background categories make it easier to look for a surface treatment without browsing the whole page library.

## Using it with agents

There is no published MCP, API, CLI, registry, llms.txt or skill. Use the visual previews as references, then describe the layout, palette, type and motion you want. The prompt-copy action is available on individual gallery entries.

## Watch out for

- Treat the landing pages as prompt-led examples rather than verified production sites; a card preview is not proof of the design's live implementation.
- Free assets require an account. Premium prompts and assets need an active monthly or lifetime plan; a monthly subscription only licenses new downloads while active.
- The licence permits embedding assets in end products but bars redistribution as standalone assets, templates or packs.

## Reusable ideas

- Browse full-page and single-section entries separately so an expressive hero does not become a whole-page style prescription.
- Preview a moving background behind the intended content before adopting it; the page offers named examples and direct preview controls for this comparison.
- Keep the prompt, background and foreground composition as separate decisions so a visual effect does not compete with the headline.
