# Curations Supply

## What it is

Curations Supply is a directory of other curated sites: design galleries, type and colour resources, UX references and component libraries. It is run by Panda Network, which also runs Best Designs on X and Collect UI. At review it listed 286 entries, 17 of them featured. Each card shows the site's logo, a one-line description, a screenshot and its domain. Examples include SEESAW, Godly, Httpster and Awwwards for websites, Uncut and MaxiBestOf for typefaces, Flat UI Colors and InclusiveColors for colour, Laws of UX, Practical UI and Microcopy for UX, and Magic UI, React Bits and Tailkits for components. It is a SvelteKit app on Supabase.

## When to open it

Open it when you are hunting for the right niche gallery (OG images, headlines, social proof, video game UI, wireframes) rather than for a design itself. It works as a quick index of what curation sites exist, next to broader directories.

## Most useful

- **Niche galleries you may not know**, such as collections of website headlines, social-proof sections, OG images, game interfaces and wireframes
- **Featured and Latest additions rows** for a fast look at what is new
- **Tag search** with suggestions, plus favourites once you sign in
- **Weekly newsletter** of new picks
- **Screenshot on each card**, so you can judge the look of a site before leaving

## Using it with agents

There is no API, MCP server or llms.txt, and the llms.txt path only returns the app shell. The listings load from the database after the page opens, so a plain fetch sees almost nothing. Browse it yourself and give the agent the links you pick, or use a directory that publishes an llms.txt.

## Watch out for

- Sponsored "Network Sponsors" cards (Mobbin, Framer, Readymag, Adobe and others) sit next to organic entries, and at least one sponsor also appears in the main list
- A few entries have little to do with design, such as a digital planner and a minimalist-lifestyle site
- Descriptions are only a few words long, so you have to open each site to judge it
- Content is rendered in the browser only, which makes it hard to cite or archive

## Reusable ideas

- A directory of directories helps people find the right specialist gallery instead of one more generic one
- Show the site's logo and a screenshot together on each card, so both brand and look come through
- Put a small featured row above the full list to point newcomers to the best starting places
- Show a row of known curators' avatars as social proof, without claiming more than it proves
