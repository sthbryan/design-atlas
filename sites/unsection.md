# Unsection

## What it is

Unsection indexes live-site captures one block at a time. It separates website examples from community submissions, and labels each card with a section type, source site and style or industry tags. The current home page shows categories including Hero, Feature, Footer, Navbar, Pricing, FAQ and Contact.

## When to open it

Use it when a page needs a specific section and you want to compare real implementations without scanning many full websites. It is also useful for visual directions such as large type, visible borders, dark palettes or bento layouts.

## Most useful

- **Section types**: the left category list links to stable routes such as [Hero](https://www.unsection.com/category/hero-section-design), [Feature](https://www.unsection.com/category/feature-section-design), [Pricing](https://www.unsection.com/category/pricing-section-design) and [Navbar](https://www.unsection.com/category/navbar-design).
- **Style and type filters**: use the homepage's Style and Type controls to narrow captures by visual treatment and the kind of product or industry.
- **Individual records**: open a card such as [CoFounder Agent Tools Feature Section](https://www.unsection.com/section/cofounder-agent-tools-feature-section). Its large capture makes the layout, type scale, palette and image treatment easy to study; Visit leads to the featured source site.
- **Search**: the search field helps locate names or content visible in the section captures. The homepage also announces text search inside screenshots.

## Using it with agents

No MCP, API, `llms.txt` or agent export was visible in this review. An agent can use the browser to filter and open public captures, then follow Visit to the original site. Describe the composition seen in the capture and check the source at the target viewport before adapting it.

## Watch out for

- The free homepage is available without a subscription, while section categories and broader library access are listed under paid Pro and Lifetime plans; prices can change.
- A capture shows one section at a time and may not represent the current source page. Visit the original site to verify responsive behavior, type and motion.
- The gallery does not grant reuse rights to the featured websites' copy, images or visual identity. Treat the captures as references.

## Reusable ideas

- Indexing by section type makes same-purpose comparisons quick; style and industry tags add a second route into the material.
- Show a section capture with its source name and a direct visit link so people can move from a visual reference to the live implementation.
- A captured section can communicate structure and content density at a glance, while the source page supplies context and interaction.
