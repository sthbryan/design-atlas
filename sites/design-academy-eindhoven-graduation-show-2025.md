# Design Academy Eindhoven Graduation Show 2025

## What it is

Design Academy Eindhoven's Graduation Show 2025 page introduces the annual show and connects it to the academy's BA and MA graduation project archive. The show page uses DAE's pale lilac and black editorial interface, with an image carousel and large project-show heading; the project archive has filters for programme, year, department and award. At review, the show described 200 graduate projects. The photographs and work in those pages are student projects, distinct from the academy's interface and exhibition identity.

## When to open it

Open it when you want to browse graduation work across disciplines and formats, or study how an event overview can lead into a longer-lived archive with filters and individual project records.

## Most useful

- **Show presentation**: a compact event overview, image sequence and link to the project archive.
- **Project archive**: filter graduation work by programme, year, department and award.
- **Individual project pages**: *Who Stole the Sky?* by Ze Qiu is a verified 2025 example, described as an experimental film about environmental narratives and Inner Mongolian herders.
- **Exhibition context**: the 2025 event explains its rooms and atmospheres, from time-based works to participatory projects and material installations.

## Using it with agents

No MCP, API, CLI or agent guide surfaced in the show pages at review. The browser could not retrieve a direct `/llms.txt` check, so its availability could not be confirmed. Use the archive filters to choose a project, then give the agent its page URL and your own notes on medium, information hierarchy or visual treatment.

## Watch out for

- This is a historical 2025 exhibition page. The top navigation promotes the current 2026 show.
- The page links to a separate project archive; the show overview itself is not a virtual walk-through of every work.
- DAE's disclaimer says its website text, layout, images and other items are protected by copyright and bars copying or adapting them without written permission.

## Reusable ideas

- Connect an event's art direction and spatial story to a durable, filterable archive of the projects it presents.
- Give each project a stable page with its maker, title, medium and a short account of its intent.
- Filter by both year and department so an archive can remain useful across graduating cohorts.
