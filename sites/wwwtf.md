# wwwtf.site

## What it is

wwwtf.site is a young, hand-picked gallery of strange, playful and heavily interactive websites, built in Framer by the designer and curator who signs as "aaali" (äli), who also runs Browse.cool and Sections.wtf. At review time it listed 25 sites, each with its own short page, a "you might also like" row and a link out. A weekly newsletter started in July 2026, and a submission form says reviews take one to two weeks and not everything is accepted.

## When to open it

When a brief asks for something memorable rather than conventional, such as a launch site, a studio portfolio or a campaign page, and you want to see how far WebGL, physics and odd navigation can be pushed before usability breaks.

## Most useful

- A mix of WebGL studio work (Lusion, Igloo Inc., Dimension, a shader studio) and product sites that took a risk with their launch page.
- Nostalgic and hand-made entries, such as Cameron's World, alongside polished agency sites, which widens the range beyond one aesthetic.
- Small enough to scan in a few minutes, with every entry linked straight to the live site.

## Using it with agents

No export, feed, `llms.txt` or API; the only structured data is Framer's own search index. Use it manually: open a site, note the one interaction you want (a cursor effect, a 3D scene transition, a scroll-driven scene) and describe it to the agent in plain terms.

## Watch out for

- Very small catalogue at review time; entries carry no tags, descriptions or credits beyond the name and URL.
- Many featured sites are heavy WebGL experiences that perform poorly on low-end devices; treat them as ideas, not templates.
- Framer-built, so the gallery pages themselves need JavaScript.

## Reusable ideas

- Pair each entry with a "you might also like" row so a short list still invites exploration.
- State the review time and acceptance odds next to the submission form.
- Borrow a single signature interaction from an experimental site rather than its whole structure.
