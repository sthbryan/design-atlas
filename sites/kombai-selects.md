# Kombai Selects

## What it is

Kombai Selects is a curated feed of interface and motion work from X, Dribbble and Behance. The feed separates picks by source and links each card to a detail view with its creator, source post, tags and a short visual description. It is useful for collecting visual directions across web and product UI, including hero sections, dashboards and mobile screens.

## When to open it

Open it when a brief needs a specific visual direction for an interface, especially when you want to compare different treatments of the same surface such as a landing hero, analytics dashboard or mobile flow.

## Most useful

- **Source filters**: browse X, Dribbble or Behance separately, or move through the combined feed.
- **AI Agent Website by Imran Hossen**: the [Kombai Selects detail](https://kombai.com/selects/designs/ai-agent-website-27701680/) is a static landing-page shot; study how the warm light on a 3D portrait balances a large headline and compact metric cards. The detail links to the original Dribbble post.
- **N233 Circular Hero Reel by Basit A. Khan**: find it in the [main feed](https://kombai.com/selects/) to study an orbiting-image hero and its motion treatment.
- **Cross-links to the Kombai Gallery**: category cards lead to separate interface references intended for remixing; these are distinct from the selected third-party shots.

## Using it with agents

The reviewed pages expose static references and links to the original posts. They do not expose a documented MCP, API, CLI, `llms.txt` or other agent channel. Brief an agent with the reference URL and the particular composition or interaction to adapt; the image itself is inspiration, not a reusable page or asset.

## Watch out for

- A selected image or reel is a creator's post, not necessarily a live, working interface. Follow the source link before assuming a pattern behaves as shown.
- The picks span different formats and fidelity levels, from polished interface shots to motion concepts and renders.
- No reuse rights are stated for selected work. Do not copy images or layouts into a product as if they were licensed assets.

## Reusable ideas

- Keep the creator and original-source link beside each visual so references remain attributable.
- Tag individual examples by interface surface and visual treatment, so a broad feed can still answer a specific design question.
- Pair a visual with a short description of its composition and interaction instead of relying on a thumbnail alone.
