# Reeoo

## What it is

Reeoo is a searchable reference archive for web pages, apps, App Store screenshots, event cards, icons and creative tools. At review it listed 4,398 websites, 341 apps, 525 screenshot sets, 335 events and 532 icons. The white home page places a large black sans-serif title above central search, format counts and featured references; a Style Combos column gives quick paths into visual directions.

Entries connect the capture to the live source and carry tags across colour, industry, style, typography, layout, page type and interaction. The [Rene reference](https://reeoo.com/website/rene/) has a large vertical capture and this metadata. Its linked [live site](https://rene.co/) opens on a white two-column hero, with a large grey sans-serif headline on the left and a black line illustration on the right. Study that page as the source design; the archive's own home page and detail layout are separate references.

## When to open it

Open Reeoo when you need to find comparable work by product area or visual decision, especially across more than one format. Search or filter by industry and style for a direction, by colour or type for a visual system, and by page type or interaction when the question is about layout or behavior.

## Most useful

- Start with a content type—Website, App, Screenshot, Event, Icon or Tool—to browse at the right level of detail.
- Use the Style Combos to enter through a mood such as Modern, Illustration, Playful, Elegant or Soft UI.
- Open a record such as [Rene](https://reeoo.com/website/rene/) to compare its capture with tags for industry, style, typography, layout, page type and interaction, then follow the original-site link.
- Browse the app and store-screenshot sets for visual references that a website-only gallery will not cover.

## Using it with agents

No MCP, API, CLI or agent-specific channel was visible on the reviewed pages. The archive is usable through a browser: search the public index, open a record, and follow its live-site link. Keep the record's tags and screenshot observations distinct from what you confirm on the current source site.

## Watch out for

- Captures are static references; they do not establish how a current product behaves or whether it meets accessibility needs. Check the live source for those questions.
- A screenshot's colour, typography or interaction tags describe the archived example. Treat them as useful labels, then confirm the visual details in the capture and current site.
- Reeoo marks its material all rights reserved. Use captures for visual study; no general permission to copy or redistribute them was stated.

## Reusable ideas

- Organize references across colour, industry, device and content type so people can move from a design question to comparable examples.
- Give each record several distinct facets—such as typography, layout, page type and interaction—instead of flattening every reference into a single style tag.
- Keep a prominent link to the live source beside its capture and descriptive metadata, so the archive supports both quick comparison and deeper inspection.
- Pair a large visual capture with related references that share a product area or style, making it easy to continue a focused search.
