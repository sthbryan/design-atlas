# McCafé Visual Identity Refresh

## What it is

McDonald’s announcement of McCafé’s 2026 visual refresh, with images of the new beverage range, packaging and storefront signage. The article names a modernized script wordmark, refined gold and coffee-cherry primary colours, brighter secondary colours, playful food photography and packaging patterns.

## When to open it

Open the story to study how a familiar sub-brand can stretch from coffee into a broader drinks range while retaining a recognizable wordmark and a repeatable palette across packages and retail displays.

## Most useful

- **Wordmark and palette**: compare the handwritten McCafé lettering with the clean display type and deep burgundy, yellow and pale aqua used around the drinks.
- **Retail application**: the storefront image places large beverage photography and contrasting promotional panels together on real window signage.
- **Packaging and photography**: the lineup uses distinct drink colours and toppings as the visual focus, supported by repeated marks and pattern details.

## Watch out for

- This page is a launch announcement from 2026. It documents the refresh, but does not establish which markets currently use each application.
- The story includes promotional copy. Use the visible design decisions as reference, not its claims about customer preferences or product quality.
- McDonald’s media asset terms reserve the images for editorial contexts; do not extract them for reuse.

## Reusable ideas

- Keep the core wordmark and primary colours stable while using secondary colours to distinguish adjacent product variants.
- Show packaging and in-store signage together to make a visual system’s real-world range easy to compare.
- Let product photography carry flavour and variety while repeated type and pattern cues connect the applications.
