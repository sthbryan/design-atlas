# 3dicons

## What it is

3dicons is an open-source library of 1,500+ hand-crafted, rendered 3D icons, ready to use without required attribution. Under CC0 licence, icons are free for personal and commercial projects.

## When to open it

Open 3dicons when you need icons with visual depth and modern 3D character. It's perfect for contemporary interfaces, dashboards, productivity applications, and any project that wants that three-dimensional style.

## Most useful

- **1,500+ rendered icons**: fire, rocket, settings, camera, calendar, chat, shield, and more
- **Online editor**: customize colors, angles, and remix icons on the fly
- **Figma integration**: official plugin for direct access from Figma
- **Featured collections**: social, Christmas, V1 themes, and more
- **CC0 licence**: no attribution required, commercial use permitted
- **Pro version**: early access to new icons

## Using it with agents

Icons can be downloaded as SVG or PNG. AI agents can modify them, change colors, or combine them into new compositions. Figma plugin enables programmatic access.

## Watch out for

- Icons are optimized for medium sizes (32-256px); at very small sizes they may lose clarity
- 3D aesthetic may not fit all design styles
- Although CC0, some projects may have recommended attribution policies for professionalism

## Reusable ideas

- Category icons for navigation
- Illustrations for empty states
- Decorative elements for landing page sections
- Feature icons for product comparisons
- 3D interface elements for modern dashboards
