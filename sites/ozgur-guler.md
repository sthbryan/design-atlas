# Özgür Güler

## What it is

Özgür Güler's portfolio presents itself as a small pixel-art village. On first visit, a framed welcome panel introduces the maker, shows illustrated destinations for projects, social links and a gallery, and offers a separate professional view.

## When to open it

Use the homepage as a reference for turning a personal portfolio into a game-like setting. It is useful when a playful opening needs to coexist with direct access to a conventional CV and project list.

## Most useful

- A warm cream welcome card with layered terracotta borders stands out against the dark blue-violet page.
- Three small illustrated building cards make Projects, Social and Gallery feel like destinations in one consistent world.
- The card explains WASD and arrow-key movement beside a large exploration action, so the game metaphor has a visible starting point.
- Turkish and English controls, sound control and the professional-view link remain grouped at the top edge.

## Using it with agents

No published agent channel was found. Share the homepage and ask an agent to map the welcome card's navigation, language and sound controls; use the original artwork only as a visual reference.

## Watch out for

- The opening is a game interface, so make the conventional CV and projects route easy to spot for visitors who need information quickly.
- The reviewed first screen depends on pixel art and a dark full-page field; check that the actual village content loads before relying on it as a live example.
- No reuse licence for the pixel art or other portfolio content was stated.

## Reusable ideas

- Represent a small set of portfolio destinations as illustrated places, with labels and descriptions that remain understandable without the metaphor.
- Put movement instructions next to the action that starts the experience.
- Offer a plain professional route beside an expressive interactive introduction.
