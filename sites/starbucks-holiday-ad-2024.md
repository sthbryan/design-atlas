# Starbucks Holiday Ad (2024)

## What it is

Starbucks EMEA’s 2024 holiday campaign film, presented in a story that points out visual details across its stop-motion scenes. The ad follows a character through home, grocery-store and café sets, with the Siren appearing as a background drawing and toy.

## When to open it

Open the story and its embedded film to study how repeated brand cues can sit inside a narrative environment. It is most useful for stop-motion art direction, detailed miniature sets and the placement of subtle marks and product colours across shots.

## Most useful

- **Colour continuity**: the main character’s green jacket and other green props carry the brand colour through different settings, while red seasonal cups create smaller accents.
- **Brand cameos**: the Siren appears as set dressing and a toy rather than dominating every frame, rewarding attention while the character remains the narrative focus.
- **Miniature environments**: the grocery aisle and café include dense shelves, food and merchandise that make each scene feel inhabited.
- **Stop-motion detail**: the linked film’s handcrafted figures and sets give the campaign a tactile look; the story also links to a production article about the technique.

## Watch out for

- This is a historical 2024 holiday campaign. Its seasonal products, store dressing and media context may no longer be current.
- Brand placements and product details are campaign-specific. Reuse the compositional ideas without copying the Siren, Starbucks marks or product packaging.
- The article is written as promotional content; focus on visible choices in the film rather than commercial claims.

## Reusable ideas

- Carry one brand colour through a character or prop so a long narrative stays visually connected across scene changes.
- Place secondary identity cues in the environment to reward close viewing while keeping the central action clear.
- Build a miniature world with repeated small details that support the story instead of relying on a single logo reveal.
