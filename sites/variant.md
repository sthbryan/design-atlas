# Variant

## What it is

Variant is a prompt-led interface generator presented as an endless stream of designs. Its [homepage](https://variant.com/) places a dark prompt composer beside a masonry-like field of embedded, interactive mini-prototypes. This makes the site itself a reference and a discovery surface: inspect the visible experiments for interface ideas, then use the prompt and style controls to explore a direction of your own.

## When to open it

Open the [homepage](https://variant.com/) when you want a quick visual prompt for new product-interface directions or a varied set of small examples to browse. Scroll the live tiles and inspect their controls before writing a prompt. The Surprise me action routes to sign-in with a suggested prompt; it does not show a generated result without an account.

## Most useful

- The homepage shows a grid of embedded prototypes rather than a static gallery card. Examples observed during review included a bike-battery status panel with range and charge controls, a weather panel over a Golden Gate Bridge image, and a movie-rating interface with selectable film posters ([live examples](https://variant.com/)).
- The Bike Battery tile uses segmented cyan charge bars, a large percentage, a remaining-range figure, and two compact status cards. The movie-rating tile presents film art as a selection surface with a separate rating action.
- The page offers a prompt field, image attachment, style selection, and a Surprise me route. Accessing Surprise me moved to account sign-in with the prompt prefilled in the URL; pricing also redirected to sign-in when opened directly.

## Using it with agents

I found no public MCP, API, CLI, `llms.txt`, or agent-integration documentation on the reviewed public pages. The homepage asks for a prompt and offers a style control, but a public generation workflow could not be inspected without signing in.

## Watch out for

- The prototype grid loads asynchronously. Several cards remained empty after waiting, while others loaded after an additional delay. Recheck a tile before deciding it is broken or treating a partial grid as the complete catalog.
- Price information was not publicly visible in this review: the [pricing route](https://variant.com/pricing) redirected to sign-in.
- The [terms](https://variant.com/terms-and-privacy) limit use of the service to personal, non-commercial use and restrict copying or scraping. They also prohibit using service outputs or other service data to train or improve AI systems. That specific restriction does not establish broader reuse rights for generated outputs; treat the examples as look-only inspiration unless a separate permission says otherwise.

## Reusable ideas

- For the site's own presentation, note the fixed dark composer beside a tall, varied grid of demos. The sparse heading and dim secondary copy leave the prototypes as the primary visual material.
- Browse examples by interaction as well as appearance: a status control, a selectable poster row, and image-led weather composition reveal different ways to turn a single card into a working miniature.
- Let the examples suggest a direction, then write a fresh prompt for your own content and states. The site provides visual stimuli, while its public pages do not expose a browsable tag taxonomy or verified agent endpoint.
