# Landing Love

## What it is

Landing Love is a gallery of landing pages and websites where each entry is a full-page video of the live page scrolling and animating, not a still screenshot. At review time it listed 2,156 sites, added by hand, each linking to the live site and crediting the studio or designer behind it. It sits alongside partner galleries Lapa Ninja, UIStore.Design and Bookmarks.Design. The operator is not named beyond the brand.

## When to open it

When the thing you need to study only exists in motion: a loading sequence, type that settles as you scroll, a 3D scene that turns with the page, a horizontal-scroll story. It is most useful for studio, portfolio, agency and product-launch pages, which dominate the collection.

## Most useful

- **48 categories** by industry and style (Minimal, 3D Website, Portfolio, Studio, Agency, SaaS, AI and more), plus Dark Mode, Light Mode, Gradient and Retro style tags
- **Tech collections**: GSAP (191), WebGL (134) and ThreeJS (74) sites, and platform filters for Webflow, Framer and Wix Studio. These are the closest thing to an "animation type" index
- **Horizontal Scroll** category (132 sites) for a pattern that is hard to judge from stills
- **Timeline**: 103 sites recorded more than once, so you can compare a redesign against the previous version
- **Studio pages** that group every credited site by the same maker

## Using it with agents

There is no API or MCP, but the site is easy for agents to read. The `llms.txt` describes the structure and the robots file explicitly allows AI crawlers. There are five sitemaps (pages, posts, videos, taxonomies and authors), an RSS feed of new additions, and schema.org VideoObject data on every entry. Agents can look up references by category or year. Watching the recordings and describing the motion is still a human job.

## Watch out for

- Pages carry ads, sponsor cards and affiliate template promotions mixed into the grid
- The privacy page says there are no accounts, yet the header shows a sign-in and entries offer "Save to board"
- Videos show behaviour but carry no timing notes or code; written notes appear only on some entries
- The content leans heavily towards portfolio and studio sites, which don't reflect typical product marketing constraints

## Reusable ideas

- Judge a landing page by a full recording, since the loader and scroll choreography are often the point
- Tag references by build technology (GSAP, WebGL, Three.js) as well as by industry, so you can find ones that match your stack
- Keep older captures when a site is redesigned to track how a brand's web presence changes
- Credit the studio on every reference so a good find leads to more of their work
