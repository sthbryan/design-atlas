# PsudoKit

## What it is

Pulkit Saraf's personal portfolio collects a short biography, project and work links, writing, and GitHub activity.

## When to open it

Open it for a dark, text-led portfolio that needs personality without relying on a large hero illustration or dense cards.

## Most useful

The page centers a narrow text column on a near-black canvas. Small, widely spaced navigation labels include keyboard shortcuts, while delicate curved lines cross the background and faint bat silhouettes add personal detail. A thin rule and inset left border distinguish the short personal notes; a rounded contribution graph anchors the lower part of the column in a muted green palette.

## Using it with agents

No agent-facing integration was found. The projects, work history and writing are linked from the visible navigation.

## Watch out for

The line art, bat motifs and anime image are personal decoration. Dark gray body text also depends on the very dark background for its contrast.

## Reusable ideas

- Keep a dark portfolio readable with a limited number of text sizes and a clear central measure.
- Add identity through subtle background line art rather than filling the hero with cards.
- Give short personal details a left rule and generous vertical spacing to make them read as notes.
