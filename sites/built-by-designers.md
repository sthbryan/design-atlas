# Built by Designers

## What it is

Built by Designers is a compact, curated directory of launched digital products. At review it listed 82 projects across seven categories, including Creative Tools, Development, Productivity, Lifestyle, Collections, Discovery and Play. Its own interface is a quiet, pale list with small product marks and oversized names; selecting an entry opens a short description and a direct link to the product, so the directory's presentation stays distinct from the work it features.

## When to open it

- When you want to discover small, design-led products rather than browse only polished agency portfolios.
- When you want a short editorial selection with category filters and a direct route to the product itself.
- When you have a product category in mind and want to follow several launches for different interaction and branding ideas.

## Most useful

- The [project list](https://builtbydesigners.com/) can be narrowed with its category controls. The labels include counts, which were current at review.
- Project details show a short description, category, curator attribution and an **Open** link. For example, [Bencho](https://builtbydesigners.com/projects/bencho/) opens the live [Bencho library](https://bencho.dev/), whose page has interactive components such as a liquid toggle, signature pad and drag controls.
- The [about section](https://builtbydesigners.com/#about) identifies the curators; the collection is selective and not a complete market index.

## Using it with agents

No MCP, API, CLI, `llms.txt` or agent guide was listed. Use a category to discover a few projects, then follow each project's direct link and ask the agent to describe the live interaction, visual hierarchy and product context it can verify. Treat the directory title and category as discovery aids, not as evidence that the linked project's current design still matches its listing.

## Watch out for

- The site shows curated projects and credits, but does not state a licence for their screenshots or visual assets. Visit the linked project and check its own terms before reusing anything.
- The catalogue is small and curator-led; its categories and featured products reflect the curators' selection.
- The directory's restrained list is its own visual design. Do not confuse it with the often very different interfaces shown in each project.

## Reusable ideas

- Use a narrow category rail and a simple list when the product names and destinations matter more than thumbnail spectacle.
- Make each listing open a concise detail view with curator credit and one clear route to the live product.
- Keep a small, handpicked product index alongside larger screenshot archives for quick discovery.
