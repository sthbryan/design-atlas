# SaaSFrame

## What it is

SaaSFrame is a paid reference library of SaaS design that covers both sides of a product: marketing websites and the in-app interface. The site cites 5,000+ examples; at review its sitemap listed about 6,100 example pages across roughly 320 SaaS companies, plus about 500 flows. It is run by a solo maker (the FAQ gives an unmake.io contact) who also sells a Figma-to-Webflow build service from the same site.

## When to open it

When one project spans a landing page and the product behind it, for example a pricing page, the sign-up flow it leads to, and the empty dashboard after that. It is also good for finding real examples of a single UI pattern, such as API key screens or audit logs.

## Most useful

- **Website categories**: 40 page types, led by landing (289), pricing (211) and about (128) at review, with rarer ones like changelog, security, comparison and affiliate pages
- **Section tags**: about 30 section types (bento grid, calculator, pricing plan comparison, infinite marquee, cookie banner)
- **Product categories**: account setup (598), onboarding (352), settings, sign-up flows and empty states
- **Pattern index**: roughly 120 UI patterns, including AI chat, AI generation state, prompt input, citations and multi-factor authentication
- **Flows** that put screens in journey order, **version comparisons** of redesigned pages, and a search over the text inside screenshots (Pro)
- **Figma files** of website pages and mobile versions (Pro)

## Using it with agents

There is no API, MCP or `llms.txt`. Screenshots and files sit behind a login. Use it as human research: pick a flow, then describe the step order and states to your agent. Paste in copy you rewrite yourself, not the text in the screenshots.

## Watch out for

- The free tier is a preview; full browsing, filters, text search and Figma files need Pro
- Prices differ between pages ($14 a month and $139 a year on the home page, $18 a month and $140 a year on the pricing page)
- The refund window is 24 hours in the terms but 7 days in the pricing FAQ
- Nothing says whether the downloadable Figma files of third-party sites may be reused

## Reusable ideas

- Link each marketing page to the product screens it leads to, so the whole funnel is visible
- Index UI patterns separately from page categories; they answer different questions
- Keep old versions of a page next to the new one to show what a redesign changed
