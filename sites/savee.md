# Savee

## What it is

Savee combines a visual-reference product at savee.com with a public marketplace of motion templates. The marketplace groups work by After Effects, Figma and Jitter, with collection pages and product pages that show full compositions and motion previews.

## When to open it

Use the marketplace to study kinetic typography, editorial layouts and product-demo motion. The public Savee reference library is a separate destination; its MCP can search public references and retrieve saved items when your plan grants access.

## Most useful

- **Titles** ([product page](https://marketplace.savee.com/products/titles)) shows oversized stacked type over monochrome fashion imagery, with the template presented beside its purchase details.
- **Category pages** ([After Effects](https://marketplace.savee.com/collections/after-effects), [Figma](https://marketplace.savee.com/collections/figma), [Jitter](https://marketplace.savee.com/collections/jitter)) make it quick to compare transition styles and title treatments.
- **Product previews** let you inspect the same visual idea in different template formats before opening the individual listing.

## Using it with agents

The Savee MCP endpoint is `https://mcp.savee.com/mcp`. Savee documents public-library search and retrieval tools, with access on paid plans; the default permissions are read-only. Write access requires a separate permission grant. The marketplace itself has no agent endpoint.

## Watch out for

- The public inspiration library may prompt for sign-in, while the template marketplace is browseable without an account.
- The reviewed Titles listing says its included imagery must be replaced for commercial work. Check each product's licence and asset terms before reuse.
- Prices and licence options can change; the marketplace's Standard, Commercial and Commercial+ tiers allow different team sizes.

## Reusable ideas

- Pair a large typographic category label with a dense but orderly preview grid to make a template catalogue feel editorial.
- Keep motion samples and purchase details together so visitors can judge the composition before reading specifications.
- Show licence scope beside the product rather than hiding team limits in checkout.
