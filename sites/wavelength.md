# Wavelength

## What it is

Wavelength presents an AI-assisted customer-success platform for account data and workflows. Its home page pairs a dark product story with a dashboard preview and bright, multicoloured route lines that run around the interface.

## When to open it

Open the [home page](https://wavelength.cx/) when a B2B dashboard needs a more distinctive frame than a row of generic feature cards. The page is a single product example, not a gallery or a source of reusable components.

## Most useful

- Study the opening composition: large centred type sits between a narrow announcement bar and a white navigation strip; a branching neon route illustration frames the account table below.
- The visible table preview makes the product concrete. Its filters and coloured tags sit inside a familiar CRM grid, while the surrounding graphic gives the otherwise dense data a memorable brand motif.
- Follow the [Integrations page](https://wavelength.cx/integrations) to see how the same product story is extended beyond the home hero.

## Using it with agents

No agent integration was found on the reviewed home page, and `https://wavelength.cx/llms.txt` returned “Not found.” Open the live pages in a browser and ask an agent to describe the layout and visual roles; do not treat the screenshots or brand graphics as licensed assets.

## Watch out for

- The product is sales-led: the primary action schedules a demo, and public pricing was not stated at review.
- The footer's [Terms & Conditions route](https://wavelength.cx/legals/terms-conditions) was available, but its body presented privacy-policy copy. No site-visual reuse terms could be confirmed.
- The page announces “Assembly is now Wavelength”; check the current brand and product details again before relying on operational claims.

## Reusable ideas

- Carry a single diagrammatic motif through a landing page and let it frame the product preview instead of competing with it.
- Keep the dashboard legible and recognisable, then use restrained tag colours to connect the product UI to the brighter marketing illustration.
