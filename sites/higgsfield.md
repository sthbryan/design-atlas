# Higgsfield

## What it is

Higgsfield's homepage is a landing page for a digital creative suite. Its visual system pairs near-black surfaces with sharp chartreuse accents, large cinematic preview cards and a dense horizontal product navigation.

## When to open it

Open it when shaping a dark, image-led landing page for a creative or media product. The useful reference is how the page puts the product identity and navigation over a large visual catalogue while keeping the primary account action easy to spot.

## Most useful

- The thin chartreuse announcement strip gives temporary messaging its own row above the persistent dark navigation.
- A compact navigation groups product areas in one line, with pricing and account actions visually separated at the right.
- Large, edge-to-edge media cards lead the page; short labels and captions sit directly beneath each preview instead of inside a crowded overlay.
- The homepage moves from broad featured media into smaller model and project cards, giving repeated image tiles a clear scale hierarchy.
- The brightest accent connects the active navigation item, sign-up controls and promotional emphasis.

## Using it with agents

The home page is the visual reference. Higgsfield also publishes an MCP workflow catalog, a CLI setup guide and API examples, but those integrations are for generating media and creative workflows, not for implementing the page. The standard `/llms.txt` address could not be opened in the browser during review.

## Watch out for

- The product navigation is wide and the promotional strip adds another fixed-height row; check how both behave at narrow widths.
- Video previews and large media cards take most of the first screen. Keep product context and the next action readable when media is slow or unavailable.
- The Terms of Use reserve rights in the Service's content. Treat the visual identity, page copy and featured media as look-only; the terms grant separate rights for generated outputs.
- Pricing changes often. At review, Starter was listed at $15/month with annual billing; check the live pricing page for current terms.

## Reusable ideas

- Give temporary promotions a distinct band above the main navigation so the product controls remain in a consistent place.
- Let cinematic previews carry the visual character, then place concise labels below them for a stable text baseline.
- Use one bright accent for the navigation state and primary actions against a restrained dark foundation.
- Shift from a few oversized previews to smaller repeated tiles to show range without making every item compete equally.
