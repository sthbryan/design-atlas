# Monash MADA Now 2025

## What it is

MADA Now is Monash Art, Design and Architecture's online collection for its 2025 graduate exhibition. The page introduces a range of work, then offers direct routes to Fine Art, Design and Architecture and a set of featured graduates. The live page layers large yellow exhibition lettering over photography; its initial view can be covered by a reconciliation notice and cookie controls. Individual student pages are separate work samples, with their own text, imagery and external portfolio links.

## When to open it

Open it when you need an example of a faculty-led student archive that starts with three clear disciplines and continues into student, course and award indexes.

## Most useful

- **Browse by faculty**: Fine Art, Design and Architecture form the top-level routes.
- **Featured graduates**: the home page links directly to a changing selection of student pages.
- **View all**: open full indexes of students, courses and awards.
- **Individual project pages**: Lucy Corcoran's profile includes *FLOJO*, a board game about menstruation education, plus related identity and campaign work. Those campaign visuals belong to the student projects, not the yellow-on-photo catalogue design.

## Using it with agents

No MCP, API, CLI or agent guide surfaced in the show pages at review; direct `/llms.txt` requests on the site and its host returned 404. Use the discipline, student and award routes in a browser, then brief the agent with a chosen project page and notes on the catalogue pattern you want to adapt.

## Watch out for

- This is the 2025 archive; the exhibition's physical venue information is also part of the page.
- A first-visit acknowledgement overlay and cookie panel can obscure the work until dismissed.
- Monash's copyright terms protect its site content and also recognise that third parties may own included material; treat student work as look-only unless its creator says otherwise.

## Reusable ideas

- Start a varied collection with a short list of broad disciplines, then offer complete indexes for visitors who know what they need.
- Connect featured students to related people and to the full student list so discovery continues beyond a single profile.
- Pair an online archive with a campus-location map when the work also belongs to a physical exhibition.
