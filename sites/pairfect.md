# Pairfect

## What it is

Pairfect presents a browser-based set of activities for long-distance couples: synchronized photo booth poses, quizzes, shared video, letters, a gratitude jar and games. The landing page embeds a two-person product demo and previews its activity screens before sign-in.

## When to open it

- When studying a landing page that lets visitors try a simulated product interaction before creating an account.
- When presenting a collection of activities as a guided date-night sequence rather than a feature list alone.

## Most useful

- **Two-screen demo**: shows a question and a choice on one partner's screen, then reflects the state on the second screen.
- **Activity previews**: each card pairs a device mockup with a short description and a route to that activity.
- **Itinerary**: groups four short activities visible at review into a suggested evening sequence.
- **Pricing**: Starter is ₹0; the page showed ₹129/month, ₹249 for three months and ₹1,999 lifetime for paid plans at review.
- **Keepsake framing**: ties game interactions to saved letters, photo strips and a shared archive.

## Using it with agents

No MCP, API, CLI, registry, `llms.txt` or agent guide was linked at review. The public page is a marketing surface with simulated demo states.

## Watch out for

- The demo uses sample identities and simulated screens; it does not establish that a live synchronized session is available without creating a room.
- The page links `/terms`, but that route returned a 404 during review. No reuse licence was stated, so treat its interface, copy and imagery as look-only.
- Prices are in Indian rupees and may change; verify the current pricing before purchase.

## Reusable ideas

- Show both sides of a shared interaction so the product's synchronization is understandable before sign-up.
- Connect individual activity cards to a sequence that answers what a user might do next.
- Explain which parts are free and which are shared between two accounts at the point of pricing.
