# Luna UI

## What it is

Luna UI is a Framer studio and template shop for startup websites. Its public catalogue mixes free and paid templates with detailed previews; the Fovea preview is a live waitlist page rather than a static mockup.

## When to open it

Open it when a startup landing page needs a cinematic hero or a product story built around large photography, responsive device mockups and scroll reveals. Use the catalogue to compare several treatments before settling on one direction.

## Most useful

- **[Templates](https://lunaui.co/templates)**: a dark gallery with a very large page title, a centered secondary line and a two-column collection of framed previews. At review, the $199 all-access offer was shown against a crossed-out $522 total; individual templates had separate prices and some were free.
- **[Fovea template](https://lunaui.co/templates/fovea)**: a live example for an AI visual-search waitlist. Its landing page puts an eye close-up inside a movable focus frame, adds small confidence labels around it and lets a translucent wordmark fill the lower hero. The next sections use full-bleed photography and numbered steps.
- **[Fovea live demo](https://fovea.framer.website/)**: inspect the page itself to see the frame, waitlist, feature sequence and FAQ at full scale. The hero's drag-to-focus frame, word-swap preloader and scroll-scrubbed video are called out by the template page.

## Using it with agents

No MCP, API, CLI or agent guide surfaced in this review. Give an agent a specific live preview and describe the composition to study, such as the scale of the background wordmark or how the focus frame anchors the image. The terms permit adapting a purchased template for a project but forbid redistributing it or publishing a derivative template for resale.

## Watch out for

- The studio's own pages use a restrained near-black background, fine star specks and very large light type; separate that presentation from the visual system of each template.
- Template prices and availability vary. Confirm the current listing before buying.
- At review, the catalogue showed all-access at $199 with $522 crossed out, while the Fovea detail page showed $99 for all-access; confirm the checkout price.
- Images, layouts, animations and code remain protected; a public preview is permission to inspect, not to reuse its assets.

## Reusable ideas

- Frame one photographic detail with a movable crop and attach concise labels to the part in focus.
- Let one oversized word sit behind the product image, then reveal only enough opacity to keep the interface legible.
- Pair cinematic full-width imagery with small, numbered explanations so visual sections still have a clear reading order.
