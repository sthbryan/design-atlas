# Anjil

## What it is

Anjil's personal site is a developer profile with a short introduction, work history, writing and social links. The homepage uses a small monochrome photograph from Fewa Tal as its main image.

## When to open it

Use it for a quiet editorial treatment of a technical profile, especially when a portfolio should feel personal without a large project-card grid.

## Most useful

- A narrow, centered column and generous white space keep the short profile easy to scan.
- The landscape photograph is rendered as a coarse monochrome dither, giving the page texture while keeping its muted palette.
- A serif display headline contrasts with compact sans-serif biography and small, pale location and time details.
- Work history is a plain vertical list: organization, dates, role and one concise account of the work.

## Using it with agents

No published agent channel was visible at review. Give an agent the homepage or the `/blog` route and ask it to describe the observed type hierarchy, image treatment or work-history layout.

## Watch out for

- The small, pale metadata is deliberately quiet; preserve readable contrast when adapting the treatment.
- The photograph and written content belong to the author; no reuse licence was stated.

## Reusable ideas

- Use one personal photograph with a distinctive treatment instead of a row of generic project thumbnails.
- Keep work entries concise and aligned around dates, organizations and roles.
- Place small live context, such as location and local time, after the primary introduction so it supports rather than competes with it.
