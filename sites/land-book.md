# Landbook

## What it is

Landbook (land-book.com) is one of the longest-running website design galleries. It is run by Landbook Sp. z o.o. in Poland, and a curator reviews every entry before it is published. The Pro page cites 20,000+ website examples and 200,000+ categorised sections. Next to the gallery it has motion and OG image collections, a headlines view, a marketplace of Framer and Webflow templates (with affiliate links), and an experts directory where the designers behind featured sites can be hired.

## When to open it

When you need depth rather than a quick feed: many years of landing pages, portfolios and online stores, with section crops you can filter by colour or style. It is also a place to find the freelancer or studio behind a site you like.

## Most useful

- **Sections library** for 14 types: hero, value proposition, features, how it works, testimonials, brand logos, stats, pricing, resources, FAQ, CTA, footer, about us and case study
- **Filters** by category (landing page, portfolio, e-commerce, blog), industry, style, platform and colour, with a setting for how strictly colours match
- **Motion** and **OG image** collections, plus old site versions and mobile previews on Pro
- **Boards** for saving references (three on the free plan) and a copy-to-Figma feature
- **Experts directory** that links featured work to agencies and freelancers

## Using it with agents

The `llms.txt` maps the gallery, section, template and expert URLs and asks you to credit the designer when citing an entry. The robots file names AI crawlers and lets them read content pages. The rest of the site sits behind Cloudflare, which blocks plain HTTP clients, and the terms forbid using its content or data for AI training or commercial reuse. Agents can point people to the right section page, but shouldn't scrape it.

## Watch out for

- Free access is limited on categories, sections, motion, headlines, OG images and search results
- The Pro page's header says $9 a month while the plan card shows $6 a month billed yearly
- Screenshot downloads are a Pro feature; the images still belong to the featured sites
- Template listings are affiliate links

## Reusable ideas

- Crop every featured site into labelled sections so a hero or footer search is a filter, not a scroll
- Let people set how closely a colour must match when filtering by colour
- Link featured work to the people who made it, so inspiration leads to hiring
