# Chloé Maillot

## What it is

Chloé Maillot's portfolio presents her design-engineering practice through a brief introduction, dated notes, a component example and a loose grid of personal illustrations. The homepage also offers English/French and light/dark switches.

## When to open it

Use it for a restrained personal-site reference where editorial writing and small working components sit beside a concise professional introduction. The [PhotoCard component page](https://chloemaillot.fr/components/photo-card) gives a stable entry into the component section; the writing list includes notes on handoff, specifications and product work.

## Most useful

- Narrow text measure and generous vertical gaps keep a content-rich page calm without decorative panels.
- Notes are presented as dated, scan-friendly rows, while components have a separate section.
- Language and theme controls are visible near the identity block.
- A varied image grid adds personality below the practical work content.

## Using it with agents

No published agent channel was found. Give an agent a specific note or component URL and summarize its observed structure; the site does not present its content as a reusable component library.

## Watch out for

- The portfolio is one person's practice and visual voice, not a representative component-system catalogue.
- No reuse licence was stated for the writing, illustrations or component visuals.

## Reusable ideas

- Pair a short professional introduction with dated writing and concrete component examples.
- Keep language and theme choices close to the site identity where visitors can find them.
- Let a small set of personal images add texture after the core work information.
