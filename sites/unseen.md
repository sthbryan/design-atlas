# Unseen Studio

## What it is

Unseen Studio is a creative studio portfolio for branding, digital experiences and motion work. Its home and Projects pages place the portfolio inside a softly coloured 3D environment, then present selected work in a filterable grid.

## When to open it

Open it to study a studio portfolio that uses one continuous 3D setting to give its introduction and project index a shared visual identity. The Projects page filters work by Branding, Digital, Motion and Experiment.

## Most useful

- **Homepage**: pastel arches, stair forms and reflective water make a rendered world around the centered headline. The headline mixes an italic serif with a heavy sans face; a small white call to action and fine top navigation keep the scene in focus.
- **Projects index**: “Selected Projects” sits above All, Branding, Digital, Motion and Experiment filters. Two large image columns sit against the pale 3D setting, with each card pairing a title and work type with an arrow.
- **Composition**: the large previews, open spacing and restrained controls let the scene act as a frame for the work instead of a separate hero illustration.

## Using it with agents

No published agent channel was found. Give an agent the [homepage](https://unseen.co/) or [Projects index](https://unseen.co/projects/) and ask it to describe the relationship between the 3D environment, centered type and project grid. Treat the work and assets as visual references only.

## Watch out for

- The site opens with an audio choice; select “Enter without audio” to review it silently.
- Its 3D setting and large media can affect loading and motion comfort. Check a reduced-motion fallback and performance before adapting a similar approach.
- Individual project cards were not opened in this review, so use the index to study its presentation rather than assuming every linked project remains live.
- No reuse licence for the portfolio's design, text, images or featured work was stated.

## Reusable ideas

- Carry a single spatial motif from the homepage into the project index so both feel like parts of the same portfolio.
- Pair a contrasting serif and sans treatment inside one short headline to give a studio introduction a distinct voice.
- Make project filters explicit and place them directly above a small number of oversized previews.
