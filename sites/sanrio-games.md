# Sanrio Games

## What it is

Sanrio Games is a multilingual game brand site with an animated, softly blurred opening, a character gallery, news and a catalog. The [Sanrio Kawaii Me Live! page](https://www.sanrio-games.jp/en-us/games/sanrio-kawaii-me-live/) is a concrete title example: a full-width character hero leads into news, play modes, customization, music and download details.

## When to open it

Open it when a character brand needs to translate an existing visual identity into a game hub and individual launch pages. The homepage shows the shared brand layer, while the rhythm-game page demonstrates how a long, image-led title story can be divided into named sections.

## Most useful

- **Opening scene**: soft-focus pastel color and a large serif headline create a calm entry before the character and game imagery comes into focus.
- **Character gallery**: a dedicated gallery sits between the brand introduction and current news, giving the site a clear browse route apart from its release announcements.
- **Title hero**: the Kawaii Me Live! page fills the first view with saturated concert colors, character art, a prominent release date and registration actions grouped at the lower right.
- **Long-form sections**: play modes, customization and music each get their own labeled visual block and carousel, so the page can carry detail without turning into one continuous text column.

## Using it with agents

No published agent channel was found. Share the homepage and [Sanrio Kawaii Me Live!](https://www.sanrio-games.jp/en-us/games/sanrio-kawaii-me-live/) and ask an agent to describe how the brand intro, gallery and title-page sections guide visitors from discovery to preregistration. The illustrations, characters and other site assets are look-only; Sanrio prohibits their reproduction.

## Watch out for

- The homepage opens with an animated blur and may take a moment before its gallery and news become useful; keep a clear route to the catalog available during the introduction.
- The title page is built around licensed characters and highly specific game artwork. Its visual assets are not available for reuse.
- Sanrio Kawaii Me Live! is listed as free-to-play with in-app purchases on its title page; the brand site is free to browse.

## Reusable ideas

- Lead with a short brand statement, then give visitors a direct route to gallery, news and games.
- Separate launch information from the visual story by grouping release and registration actions in one compact panel.
- Divide a long product page into named sections, each with a distinct image or carousel and a concise purpose.
- Carry a shared color and type mood between the brand homepage and title pages while giving each title its own opening image.
