# Landdding

## What it is

Landdding is a community-submitted gallery of landing pages and website designs, with new picks every day. At review the home page showed about 2,400 designs from about 1,400 designers in 40 categories. It also lists templates for Webflow, Framer, WordPress, Wix and Squarespace. It is built by Azwedo, a small studio that runs other Webflow and design tools, and its founder is named as Bardhyl Bytyqi. According to the about page, a person reviews each submission before it goes live.

## When to open it

When you want a quick look at recent, broad web design across many industries rather than a SaaS-only set, or you want to browse by dominant colour or by the platform a site was built on.

## Most useful

- **40 categories**, from SaaS, AI and startup to architecture, charity and weather
- **Daily and weekly feeds**, plus a weekly roundup newsletter of top-voted designs
- **Browse by colour and tag** (sans serif, software, service and similar), and "similar to" pages that group lookalike sites
- **Designer profiles** that credit the submitter and collect their other entries
- **Templates by platform**, a free landing page analyser tool and a yearly state-of-landing-pages report

## Using it with agents

The `llms.txt` describes the URL structure (`/l/` for entries, `/c/` for categories, `/u/` for designers), points to a sitemap index and names the JSON-LD types on each page. It allows metadata and descriptions to be summarised and asks that citations link to the Landdding entry. The robots file allows every crawler. There is no API or MCP, so an agent can list and describe entries but not search the gallery.

## Watch out for

- Free access is limited on the feed, search, categories, tags, profiles and collections; PRO removes the caps and the ads
- The PRO page's structured data gives an $8 price, while the page shows $4 a month
- The terms (last updated February 2023) restrict site content to personal, non-commercial use
- Submitters include template sellers and SEO link offers, so some entries are promotional

## Reusable ideas

- Put a colour browse next to category browse; many searches start from a palette
- Show submission and designer counts on the front page to signal the gallery is active
- Let a launch be scheduled for a set day so submitters can plan around it
