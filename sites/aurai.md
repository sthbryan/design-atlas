# AURAI LAB

## What it is

A Shanghai creative studio for brand identity, websites and digital products. Its homepage uses an oversized serif wordmark inside a pale circular form, muted pink and lavender light, and widely tracked navigation. The CityWalk case study links to a public mobile-first city guide demo with an onboarding flow and seven screen states at review.

## When to open it

Use the homepage when exploring a soft, editorial direction for a creative studio or premium digital product. Open the CityWalk case when you need a concrete example of onboarding and a small mobile product presented through a branded case study.

## Most useful

- The hero layers a very large, high-contrast serif wordmark and short promise over a softly lit circular field, then keeps the studio navigation small and widely spaced.
- The work carousel names each project type and gives a one-sentence description before opening a detailed case study.
- The CityWalk case connects its product story to an independently openable live demo, rather than ending at static portfolio images.
- The live demo uses a pale pink-to-blue gradient, translucent rounded panels, and a short onboarding sequence. Its seven screen states at review cover entry, route setup, live quests, completion, journaling and night mode.

## Using it with agents

No MCP, API, CLI, `llms.txt` or agent guide was visible at review. Agents can open the public homepage and the [CityWalk demo](https://aurai-lab.github.io/aurai-citywalk/) in a browser. Treat both as visual references; no implementation licence was stated.

## Watch out for

- The homepage loader can sit over the content while assets initialize; wait for the hero and project carousel before judging the first screen.
- CityWalk begins with an onboarding panel. Its Skip and Next controls make the demo inspectable without creating an account.
- The studio describes the featured work as products and demos; use the linked live demo to distinguish an interactive example from the carousel's cover artwork.
- The Terms or an explicit reuse licence were not linked from the reviewed page. Do not reuse its copy, images or interface assets.

## Reusable ideas

- Give a studio identity one distinctive, oversized typographic moment, then let restrained navigation and supporting copy stay quiet.
- Explain each portfolio project in plain language beside its category so visitors can decide which case to open.
- Pair a product case study with a live demo and screen-by-screen states to make the interaction flow verifiable.
- Keep onboarding actions visually clear and let a visitor skip directly into the product.
