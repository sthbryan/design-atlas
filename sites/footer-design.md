# Footer Design

## What it is

Footer.design calls itself "the only footer gallery on earth": a curated gallery dedicated exclusively to website footer design.

## When to open it

- When your project's footer feels generic and you need real solutions by style (typographic, illustrative, grid, animated, dark, large type).
- When you want to gauge how dense or minimal a footer can be depending on the type of site.
- When you're looking for examples flagged "Editor's Choice" to get an already-curated quality filter.

## Most useful

- Style filters: typographic, small type, illustrative, grid, flat, animated, cards, bright, dark, large type, bold.
- An "Editor's Choice" flag highlighting footers the curating team rates most highly.
- Examples from brands and studios like Decimals, Playfolly, Yash Fataniya, Harvest Hall, The Design Society and Reality Is__, each linking directly to the source site.
- Lets you submit your own project to the gallery.

## Using it with agents

No export or MCP; use it as a visual reference: pick a footer in the style you want, describe its column structure, typographic hierarchy and elements (newsletter, socials, legal) to the agent so it replicates it in your own stack.

## Watch out for

- It's purely visual: no downloadable code or accessibility spec for the footers shown.
- Mixes organic content with a sponsored section; worth distinguishing the two when curating references.
- No licence is stated for how the screenshots may be used.

## Reusable ideas

- A niche as narrow as "footers only" can sustain a full gallery if well filtered by style.
- Manually flagging a subset as "Editor's Choice" adds a layer of curation on top of raw submission volume.
- Linking directly to the source site on every entry keeps the gallery a gateway, not a copy.
