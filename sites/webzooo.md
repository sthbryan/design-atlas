# Webzooo

## What it is

Webzooo is a gallery of website captures organized around colour and industry. Its home page pairs colour chips with a hex or colour-name search, category filters and a grid of example sites. A detail page shows a desktop capture, offers a mobile view, and lists palette swatches, type, category and similar sites.

## When to open it

Open it when a visual direction starts with a colour or palette, or when you want to compare how different industries use a similar hue. The colour controls and industry filters can be combined to narrow the examples.

## Most useful

- The home page puts nine colour choices above the gallery, with a separate custom-colour control and search field for a known hex value.
- Category pills sit between the search area and the examples, making industry browsing visible without hiding the visual grid.
- On the [Weav gallery capture](https://webzooo.com/site/weav), a large image sits beside palette, font, category and design notes; Desktop and Mobile controls make the viewport choice explicit.
- That archived Weav capture is a reference for oversized white copy on a saturated blue hero beside a phone mockup, with a pale pink action and product chat UI. I reviewed the gallery capture, not the current live Weav site.
- Similar-site links and colour collections offer a path to more examples after the first result.

## Using it with agents

No MCP, API, CLI, `llms.txt` or other agent channel was found during review. Use the site in a browser, choose a colour or category, and open an individual site's detail page.

## Watch out for

- The cards and captured pages are references; their imagery, branding and copy belong to the original sites. Treat them as look-only.
- A gallery screenshot is not proof of how the linked site looks today. Open the source website separately before using it as a current example.
- Palette and type details describe the capture and may not match a site's latest live version.
- The terms describe the gallery as educational and inspirational, and reserve the platform's own design and original content.

## Reusable ideas

- Make colour a first-class browse control, then let category filters refine the same visual grid.
- Pair a large reference image with a compact facts column so people can inspect a design and its palette without losing the image.
- Include both desktop and mobile views where the layout can change substantially by viewport.
