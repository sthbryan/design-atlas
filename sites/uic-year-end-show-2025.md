# UIC School of Design Year End Show 2025

## What it is

UIC's Year End Show 2025 is a digital exhibition of excerpts from graduating students' theses across design and computer science plus design programmes. The home page has oversized stacked type over a warm pink, orange and yellow image field. The Undergraduate and Graduate routes lead to separate catalogues, with discipline filters; additional navigation points to special projects, the exhibition and award winners. Salma Abdelhady's *Beyond the Noise* is a verified undergraduate example: a digital motion piece using immersive 3D particle simulations. Its moving particles are the student's work, distinct from the site's typographic identity.

## When to open it

Open it when you want to study a year-end exhibition that separates undergraduate and graduate work while keeping special projects and award selections at the top level.

## Most useful

- **Cohort navigation**: split undergraduate and graduate projects into separate catalogues.
- **Discipline filters**: on the undergraduate catalogue, narrow by Graphic Design, Industrial Design, Design Studies or Computer Science + Design.
- **Special projects and awards**: reach cross-cohort features and selected work from the main navigation.
- **Individual thesis excerpts**: project pages combine the student's name, title, text and a large media area; Salma Abdelhady's page presents *Beyond the Noise* with embedded motion work.

## Using it with agents

No MCP, API, CLI or agent guide surfaced in the show pages at review. A direct `/llms.txt` check was blocked by the browser, so its availability could not be confirmed. Use the cohort and discipline pages, then give the agent a specific project URL and describe the composition or motion to study.

## Watch out for

- This is a preserved 2025 show; links to previous years and newer editions live on other hosts.
- Embedded projects may include video or audio controls and can load separately from the catalogue page.
- The exhibition has no specific licence statement at review. Do not treat public visibility as permission to reuse images or student work.

## Reusable ideas

- Separate cohorts at the first navigation level, then expose discipline filters within each cohort.
- Use oversized, layered type as the exhibition's identity while keeping project descriptions readable on individual pages.
- Give special projects and award-winning work a stable route alongside the general student index.
