# Xnapper

## What it is

Xnapper is a screenshot editor for macOS and iOS. Its homepage is a useful visual reference because the hero places a finished, framed screenshot beside the editor controls that create it, and the feature sections demonstrate automatic balance, backgrounds and redaction.

## When to open it

Open the homepage when planning a capture-to-presentation flow or a product page for a visual utility. Use the in-page editor preview to study how an app can expose screenshot padding, aspect ratios, background presets and privacy redaction; it is a single product, not a gallery.

## Most useful

- [Homepage](https://xnapper.com/): inspect the dark hero, colored word emphasis, live-looking editor mockup and the feature demonstrations below it.
- [Pricing](https://xnapper.com/pricing): check current watermark, device and update terms before describing the free and paid paths.
- [Wall of Love](https://xnapper.com/love): see how the product presents customer feedback as social proof.
- [Privacy policy](https://xnapper.com/privacy): review the stated on-device OCR and data handling limits.

## Using it with agents

No MCP, API, CLI, llms.txt or dedicated agent guide was linked from the pages reviewed. Agents can use the homepage and pricing page as direct browser references, but screenshots, product UI and branding remain look-only unless the owner grants reuse permission.

## Watch out for

The homepage's animated editor and video demonstrations may take time to load; wait at least one second and check again before treating an empty frame as missing content. The app's editor illustrates Xnapper's own workflow, while the sample screenshot inside it is not a general-purpose gallery of third-party sites. Website terms and privacy copy are dated, so recheck legal details separately from the live product page.

## Reusable ideas

- Show the result and its controls together so users can understand how a finished screenshot is produced.
- Group presentation controls into visible choices for padding, balance, shadow, background and output ratio instead of hiding the whole workflow in a generic “edit” action.
- Demonstrate privacy-sensitive features such as automatic redaction alongside the output they change.
