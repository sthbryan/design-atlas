# ScreensDesign

## What it is

ScreensDesign is a searchable app reference library centered on real product screens and recorded flows. The library combines app metadata, visual filters and sequences tagged by product moment, including onboarding, paywalls, pricing and retention.

## When to open it

- When you need a sequence of mobile screens around onboarding, subscription decisions or a product action.
- When you want to compare a screen with its surrounding flow and video context.
- When you need examples grouped by UX moment rather than only by app category.

## Most useful

- The [library](https://screensdesign.com/library) has search, app cards and filters for Paywalls, Onboarding, Pricing, Growth loops and Retention. It listed 2,715 apps at review.
- The [Essayist showcase](https://screensdesign.com/showcase/essayist-academic-writing-app) shows a public strip of app screens. The observed sequence moves from blue welcome screens through role selection into writing.
- In the library, app cards label the captured chapters, including onboarding, paywall and product actions. The showcase provides a route to the full app recording, while its public screenshots let you inspect individual compositions.
- A [Claude showcase](https://screensdesign.com/showcase/claude-by-anthropic) is another concrete example, with app screenshots and a full-video entry point.

## Using it with agents

ScreensDesign documents a hosted MCP with 18 tools, available on Pro. Use it to locate relevant apps and flow moments, then inspect the actual screenshots before turning them into a visual brief. The public pricing page did not expose plan amounts during this review.

## Watch out for

- Some visible flow segments are marked Unlock Pro; Essayist's public previews do not expose every screen in the sequence.
- The displayed screenshots carry ScreensDesign branding. Treat them as look-only references: the site did not state reuse rights for its library or for the third-party app designs.
- Pricing amounts and the site's terms were not available in the public pricing and terms pages reviewed, so check them directly before subscribing or reusing material.
- App metrics and store screenshots describe captured or estimated product data, not guaranteed current behavior.

## Reusable ideas

- Read chapter labels alongside a screen strip to see how a flow shifts from introduction to product action or monetization.
- Compare welcome, role-selection and first-work screens to study how a product moves from brand color into a usable interface.
- Use pattern filters to find examples by the decision or task being designed, then inspect an entire flow for its surrounding context.
