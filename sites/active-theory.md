# Active Theory

## What it is

Active Theory is a creative studio portfolio whose opening view is an animated, near-black scene. A reflective outline and drifting points of light form the centerpiece, while compact Work and Contact controls sit at the top edge.

## When to open it

Open it when you are studying a dark, atmospheric studio introduction or need ideas for making a particle field and restrained navigation feel like one art-directed scene.

## Most useful

- **Opening scene**: scattered points gather around a central outlined mark, with pale light and color moving across its reflective surface.
- **Navigation**: Work and Contact share a small capsule at the upper right, keeping the central canvas visually prominent.
- **Work archive**: project links use short names and sit alongside the animated scene, creating a direct route from studio identity to examples.

## Using it with agents

There is no published agent integration. The `/llms.txt` route renders the portfolio rather than returning an agent text file. Ask an agent to inspect the live page for scene composition, motion and the position of the project navigation.

## Watch out for

- The initial scene is dark and motion-heavy; make sure essential navigation remains legible and usable when a visitor cannot rely on the animation.
- No public pricing or reuse licence was found on the reviewed page. Use it as visual reference only.

## Reusable ideas

- Give an abstract particle animation one clear central anchor so the movement still reads as composition.
- Keep navigation in a compact, high-contrast shape near the edge of the canvas.
- Let a small palette of reflected color provide motion and depth against a nearly black field.
