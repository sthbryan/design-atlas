# HEX Swipe File

## What it is

HEX's public reference board collects visual work the agency is exploring. At review it showed 47 pieces tagged across brand, web, motion and product, alongside a note that the collection is updated daily.

## When to open it

- When browsing a small, current selection of agency references rather than a large searchable stock gallery.
- When looking for brand or motion work with enough image area to inspect the composition directly.

## Most useful

- **Tag filters**: narrow the board to brand, web, motion or product and see each category's item count.
- **Variable tile formats**: pieces can appear in square, slide, A4 or original formats, giving wide work more room than uniform cards.
- **Credits and source links**: tiles identify the project and may open the original work.
- **Agency context**: navigation connects the board to HEX's work, clients, rejected concepts and writing.

## Using it with agents

No MCP, API, CLI, registry, `llms.txt` or agent guide was linked at review. Each reference needs to be opened and checked at its original source before relying on current behaviour.

## Watch out for

- The collected pieces are references by other designers and brands, not assets licensed for reuse.
- The filter categories overlap in spirit but are small and curated; this is not a general-purpose search catalogue.
- No licence for the board's images or reuse terms was stated on the page.

## Reusable ideas

- Let each gallery item choose a canvas ratio that suits its original composition.
- Keep a compact set of category filters above the work and show their counts.
- Credit the source in the gallery and preserve a route back to it.
