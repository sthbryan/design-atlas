# on.design

## What it is

on.design is a design community with a gallery feed: popular, recent and debut posts, collections, designer profiles, messages and jobs. Pro for individual designers ($8 a month at review) adds posting limits, scheduled posts, analytics and a place on the public hire page.

## When to open it

Open it for fresh design work and debuts from working designers, or when you need the community and jobs side of a gallery.

## Most useful

- **Debuts alongside popular**: new designers get a feed of their own, not only the top posts.
- **Community, not only gallery**: profiles, messages, collections and jobs around the work.
- **Hire page**: Pro profiles appear where clients look.

## Using it with agents

No MCP, API or agent guide is published; an agent can browse the feed in a browser.

## Watch out for

- The shown work belongs to its designers; use it as inspiration only.
- Posting and analytics depth sit behind Pro.

## Reusable ideas

- Give debuts their own feed so new work is not buried by popular posts.
- Pair the gallery with a hire page so the community earns its keep.
