# a-fresh

## What it is

a-fresh is an archive of website and component references. The website index has filters for page type, industry, builder, visual style and related traits; a separate entry presents a desktop or mobile preview, category tags and a direct Website link. Its pricing page lists an all-access Pro subscription.

## When to open it

Open it when you want to browse a site by both its purpose and visual qualities, or compare a saved desktop preview against a current live portfolio.

## Most useful

- Use the category row to narrow portfolios, ecommerce, SaaS, minimal or animated examples; separate sections also index components, pages, email and popups.
- [Viacheslav Novoseltsev's record](https://a-fresh.website/websites/viacheslav-novoseltsev) describes its categories and font and provides a desktop preview. The linked [live portfolio](https://vshslv.com/) opens with a full-screen portrait video, translucent top navigation and small metadata columns over the image.
- The detail view exposes desktop and mobile preview modes and lets you follow its Website link to the source.

## Using it with agents

No MCP, API, `llms.txt` or agent-specific interface was visible on the reviewed pages. Public records can be browsed directly; the site lists additional all-access membership features for paid members.

## Watch out for

- The record's Website link for Viacheslav led to a missing page on the gallery domain during review; the source site was reached directly at `vshslv.com` and matched the record's preview.
- The gallery states that screenshots belong to their respective owners. No general reuse licence was stated, so treat previews and linked sites as look-only references.
- Pro all-access was listed at $6 per month billed yearly ($72 per year) at review; check current terms and pricing before subscribing.

## Reusable ideas

- Combine multiple descriptive tags with explicit desktop and mobile preview controls.
- Keep record metadata beside a large preview, with a direct link to the live source.
