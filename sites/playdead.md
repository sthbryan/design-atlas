# Playdead

## What it is

Playdead's studio homepage uses a near-black canvas, a small uppercase navigation and a short studio introduction above a wide, desaturated game scene. Links to [LIMBO](https://playdead.com/games/limbo/) and [INSIDE](https://playdead.com/games/inside/) lead to restrained title pages. INSIDE places its large red wordmark over dark imagery, followed by store links, trailers, screenshots and short animated loops.

## When to open it

Open it to study a quiet, cinematic game-studio introduction or a media-led game detail page that lets imagery and large type carry the identity.

## Most useful

- **Homepage**: a concise studio paragraph precedes one wide landscape image; the minimal header and social links leave the scene room to breathe.
- **Title page**: INSIDE pairs a huge wordmark and near-black background with a compact purchase list beneath it.
- **Media sequence**: the page groups trailers, stills and short loops into labeled sections, making the material easy to scan beyond the opening hero.
- **Typography**: narrow uppercase labels and large title lettering sustain the restrained tone across pages.

## Using it with agents

No published agent channel was found. Ask an agent to compare the home page and INSIDE page for image scale, title placement, store-link hierarchy and the transition into media sections. The footer says all rights are reserved; game art, video and animation are look-only.

## Watch out for

- The dark, low-contrast imagery and narrow uppercase face depend on careful sizing and contrast; keep navigation and body copy easy to read.
- The screenshots and loops show protected game material. Study the composition without reusing those images or animations.
- The studio page lists retailer links; current game prices are set by those storefronts and were not stated on the reviewed pages.

## Reusable ideas

- Place a short studio statement before the hero so the image has a clear context.
- Let one oversized title label anchor a dark game page, then keep purchase links together in a quieter section.
- Label trailers, screenshots and animated loops separately to make a long visual page easier to browse.
