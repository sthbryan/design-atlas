# Cardinal

## What it is

Cardinal presents a revenue platform built around AI agents and sales playbooks. The live site uses a warm paper-like grid, handwritten annotations and sketched metric cards to soften an otherwise conventional SaaS story.

## When to open it

When a B2B product needs an approachable brand without losing structure, or when comparing hand-drawn accents with a precise grid and editorial serif headline.

## Most useful

- [Home](https://www.trycardinal.com/) — inspect the cream grid, annotated hero, sketched metric cards and the product's four numbered steps.
- [Playbooks](https://www.trycardinal.com/playbooks) — browse the linked sales examples for the site's content-card treatment.
- The queued address [trycardinal.ai](https://trycardinal.ai/) redirects to the canonical `.com` homepage at review.

## Using it with agents

The public pages are browser-readable. I did not find a public `llms.txt`, API, CLI, MCP server or installable agent skill during this review.

## Watch out for

- The handwritten details are a specific brand voice; too many could make a different product feel informal or cluttered.
- The product pages emphasize demos and playbooks; no public price or content reuse licence was found.
- Use the canonical `.com` URL because the queued `.ai` address redirects there.

## Reusable ideas

- Combine a faint grid with hand-drawn notes and small infographics to give a sales product a crafted feel without abandoning alignment.
- Put brief sketches around the headline as secondary proof or decoration, keeping the main copy centered and easy to scan.
- Translate an iterative product process into four short numbered steps, then introduce deeper playbooks as the next route.
