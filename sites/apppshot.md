# Apppshot

## What it is

Apppshot is a collection of App Store screenshot sets shown side by side so people designing their own can study how real apps present themselves. It covers 106 apps at review, searchable by name, category or colour, with categories such as productivity, health, AI, photo and social.

## When to open it

Open it when you are designing App Store screenshots and want to compare how real apps in your category frame their features.

## Most useful

- **Side-by-side sets**: full screenshot sets, not isolated images, so the story arc is visible.
- **Search by colour**: find sets that share a palette, which is how the sets compete in the store.
- **Free, no accounts**: the collection is free to use as a reference.

## Using it with agents

No MCP, API or agent guide is published; an agent can browse the sets in a browser.

## Watch out for

- The screenshots are other apps' marketing assets; use them as references, not as templates to copy.
- The collection is small (106 apps at review), so category coverage is thin in places.

## Reusable ideas

- Show screenshot sets as complete stories so the presentation logic is visible.
- Offer colour search because that is how App Store sets compete for attention.
