# ViralPost

## What it is

ViralPost is a live ranking of the design posts that crossed 1,000 likes on X: videos, shots and threads, tracked by day, week and month, hand-picked daily. Pro adds the full archive and a digest in your inbox each morning.

## When to open it

Open it for a fast read on what design work is resonating right now, rather than a curated all-time gallery.

## Most useful

- **Ranked by real engagement**: the 1,000-like threshold is the filter, so the feed tracks attention, not just uploads.
- **Day, week and month views**: see what is breaking now and what has legs.
- **Digest option**: the morning digest turns the ranking into a habit.

## Using it with agents

No MCP, API or agent guide is published; an agent can browse the ranking in a browser.

## Watch out for

- Popularity is not quality; the ranking measures likes, so treat it as a zeitgeist feed.
- The posts belong to their authors; use them as references, not assets.

## Reusable ideas

- Use an engagement threshold as the editorial filter for a fast-moving feed.
- Offer the same ranking as a morning digest for people who will not visit daily.
