# make.design

## What it is

make.design is a small AI tool from solo maker Siddharth Meena that turns a one-line prompt into a finished-looking visual: a landing page, website section, app screen, dashboard, poster, social post or ad. You refine the result in chat ("add trust badges under the headline", "switch to a dark theme") or on its canvas, and can feed it your logo, colours, tone and assets to keep it on-brand. Pricing is counted in images per month and renders, so the output is a design to hand off rather than working code.

## When to open it

Open it early in a project to get several visual directions for a landing page or campaign in minutes, before anyone opens Figma or writes code. It fits founders and marketers who need mockups, and developers who want a quick reference image to hand to a coding agent.

## Most useful

- **Showcase gallery**: public prompt-generated examples split into web design, app design, marketing and graphic design, useful as quick inspiration for heroes, footers, dashboards and portfolio pages
- **Chat edits** on a generated design, including turning one idea into matching marketing assets
- **Brand inputs** (logo, colours, tone, assets) applied across generations
- **Format range**: one prompt can target a website, ad, social post or print-style graphic

## Using it with agents

There is no MCP server, API, CLI or `llms.txt` (the path returned 404 at review), so agents cannot drive it directly. The practical route is to generate a design, export the image and give it to a coding agent as a visual reference, or to a screenshot-to-code tool. The site says designs are export-ready for developers, but it does not document a code export.

## Watch out for

- There is no permanent free tier on the pricing page, only trials. The terms still mention free plans and bar multiple free accounts
- Output is a picture of a design. Someone still has to rebuild it as accessible, responsive code
- The terms give no company name, address or governing law beyond "the jurisdiction in which make.design operates", and total liability is capped at 12 months of fees or $50
- Plan credits reset each billing cycle and don't roll over, and fees are non-refundable
- Much of the site is SEO landing pages (tool pages, comparisons, "best AI tools"), so read the showcase and pricing rather than the marketing copy

## Reusable ideas

- Show every gallery example next to the one-line prompt that produced it, so readers learn what to ask for
- Offer brand inputs (logo, colours, tone) as separate controls rather than asking users to describe them in the prompt
- Price by generation and by edit separately, so iteration costs less than starting over
