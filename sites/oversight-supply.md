# Oversight Supply

## What it is

Oversight Supply is a catalogue of Webflow and Framer templates alongside standalone page sections. The home page links to the full templates catalogue and `/sections`; the sections view lets visitors preview individual designs and copy a prompt into a project after signing in. At review, the catalogue showed 192 templates and 32 sections, including ten studio originals.

## When to open it

Open the template catalogue when you need a complete Webflow or Framer starting point. Use `/sections` when you want to study one focused block or adapt a smaller piece without browsing full-site templates.

## Most useful

- **Standalone sections**: large preview cards make it easy to scan distinct editorial directions and select a single page section.
- **Platform filters**: separate Webflow and Framer catalogues help narrow the starting point.
- **Template previews**: inspect the design before spending a credit; the terms explain that access to use a design depends on the selected template's platform licence.

## Using it with agents

No `llms.txt`, MCP, API, CLI, registry or installable skill was found during review. The sections page offers a prompt-copy action after sign-in, but this is a project handoff rather than a documented agent integration. An agent can use the public catalogue and previews as visual references.

## Watch out for

- The subscription's three credits expire monthly and do not roll over; lifetime access is a separate $499 payment at review.
- The terms allow work in personal or client projects but prohibit redistributing templates, source files, access links or account access. Check the terms and the individual platform licence before reuse.
- The pricing page's Figma availability note described planned rather than currently available files.

## Reusable ideas

- Offer sections as independent previews alongside full templates, so visitors can browse at the scale of a whole site or one block.
- Explain credit expiry and the reuse boundary next to pricing, where the purchasing decision happens.
- Let a user carry a selected visual reference into their own project with a concise prompt.
