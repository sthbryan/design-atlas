# Filmagic

## What it is

Filmagic is a launch video maker for apps, SaaS and AI products: paste your product URL and get a launch video with no editing. It also bundles free media tools: video and audio compression and conversion, image compressors and resizers, and launch-specific helpers like App Store preview resizers.

## When to open it

Open it when a launch needs a product video quickly, or when you need the free media utilities between design and shipping.

## Most useful

- **URL to launch video**: the product URL is the input, so capture and editing are automated.
- **Free tool shelf**: compressors and converters for video, audio and images, free without an account.
- **Markdown docs**: every page has a Markdown version for agents to read.

## Using it with agents

An `llms.txt` and Markdown twins of every page are the agent channels; the video maker itself is a web tool.

## Watch out for

- Paid tiers gate the launch video output; the free tools are the open part.
- Generated video still needs a look for brand fit before it ships.

## Reusable ideas

- Turn a product URL into a launch video so the site itself is the brief.
- Give away the commodity media tools and charge for the launch-specific output.
