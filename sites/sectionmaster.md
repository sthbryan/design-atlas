# sectionmaster

## What it is

sectionmaster is a curated gallery of real website footer sections: the page organizes navigation, legal links, newsletter sign-ups and brand sign-off at the bottom of live pages, collected as buildable examples. The gallery is organised by section type (hero, pricing, features, CTA, testimonials, about, footers), and the footer page is free to browse. An `llms.txt` at the site root indexes the gallery for agents.

## When to open it

Open it when a footer feels crowded or unfinished and you want to see how real sites order the same elements: nav columns, legal row, newsletter, socials, and the brand moment.

## Most useful

- **Section-scoped browsing**: footers are the whole page, not a thumbnail buried in a full-page screenshot
- **Free to browse**: the gallery costs nothing to look through
- **llms.txt**: agents can enumerate the examples without rendering every card

## Using it with agents

An `llms.txt` at sectionmaster.com lists the gallery. An agent can cite which footer patterns exist, then open the live examples to inspect the structure. There is no MCP server or API.

## Watch out for

- No stated licence: the screenshots belong to their sites, treat them as look-only
- The gallery is curated for looks; it does not grade the footers for accessibility or completeness

## Reusable ideas

- Gallery by section type, so a footer can be studied without the whole page
- An llms.txt that mirrors the gallery structure makes it browsable by agents
