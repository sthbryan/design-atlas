# Nur

## What it is

Nur's product engineer portfolio uses a quiet white page, a large watercolor pond and a short first-person introduction. The name and role appear within the sentence, with controls to change the role's typeface, weight, style and color.

## When to open it

Open it for an example of a personal introduction that makes one small text treatment interactive. The Work link leads to a separate project index, while the homepage stays focused on the person and the visual motif.

## Most useful

- A wide, softly edged watercolor pond anchors the otherwise open page without a rectangular image frame.
- The introduction reads as a sentence rather than a stack of profile cards; the role is highlighted with a handwritten face and inline formatting controls.
- A small two-choice icon control sits above the illustration, keeping navigation and work access unobtrusive.
- The muted green and blue illustration contrasts gently with near-black text and broad white margins.

## Using it with agents

No published agent channel was found. Provide the homepage and ask an agent to study the relationship between the pond illustration, text measure and inline type controls; the site does not present its visuals as reusable assets.

## Watch out for

- The inline styling interaction needs clear focus and control states so visitors understand which words can be changed.
- The low-contrast watercolor edge is atmospheric, but essential text should keep its own readable contrast.
- No reuse licence was stated for the illustration or portfolio content.

## Reusable ideas

- Give a minimal portfolio one distinctive illustration that can carry the mood without competing with the introduction.
- Make a single phrase in the introduction an interactive typography sample, and keep the controls adjacent to the phrase.
- Use whitespace and a narrow text column to make a short introduction feel deliberate.
