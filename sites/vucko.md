# Vucko

## What it is

Vucko is a Toronto motion identity studio. Its portfolio shows brand motion systems across event screens, campaigns, product and media, with short context and outcomes on case-study pages.

## When to open it

Open [Work](https://vucko.co/work) when you need to study motion as part of a brand system rather than as isolated interface animation. The homepage's project rail leads to recent case studies; [Google Next 2026](https://vucko.co/work/google-next-2026) includes a system and process panel, while [Spotify Wrapped 2025](https://vucko.co/work/spotify-wrapped-2025) is another campaign example.

## Most useful

- The homepage pairs a restrained, off-white canvas and compact type with a fixed right rail: a live city clock, pill-shaped navigation, a showreel card and a scrollable project list.
- Project pages explain the context and outcome, then place large motion and event imagery beside the studio's navigation rail. The Google Next case study expands into its motion system and process.
- The [Work index](https://vucko.co/work) is a direct way to scan identity projects by brand and open the ones that match a format such as conference, campaign or product.

## Using it with agents

No public MCP, API, CLI, skill or agent guide was found at review. Use the live case studies as visual references and describe the motion principles you observe; the studio's media is not a reusable asset library.

## Watch out for

- Some projects are marked as protected and do not expose their full case study.
- The work is high-production brand and event motion. Adapt its pacing, hierarchy and system logic to the product rather than treating the visuals as drop-in UI patterns.
- The footer reserves copyright; no reuse licence for the writing, photography or motion work is stated.

## Reusable ideas

- Keep a small persistent navigation rail visible while large project media carries the visual weight.
- Explain a motion identity through both finished brand applications and the shared rules that make them cohere.
- Use a restrained case-study shell so each client's colour, image and motion system can take over the page.
