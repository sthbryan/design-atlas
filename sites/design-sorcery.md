# DesignSorcery

## What it is

DesignSorcery is a searchable gallery of more than 638 websites at review. It pairs screenshot captures with scroll-video previews and links to the live source. The gallery itself has a white editorial layout with a large serif heading, monospaced labels, square-corner borders and screenshot cards. Its catalogue is searchable and grouped by design style, framework, color palette, industry and subcategory.

## When to open it

- When you want to scan full-page website references and then check a promising design on its live site.
- When choosing a direction based on color, framework or industry, or looking for a site with a particular interaction rhythm.
- When a screenshot is too static to judge how a long page unfolds; use the scroll-video preview where it plays.

## Most useful

- **Search and filters**: narrow the gallery by design style, framework, color palette, industry and subcategory. The color index links directly to palettes such as green, blue and monochrome.
- **Preview modes**: compare video and still captures in light and dark appearances, with desktop and mobile views when available.
- **Per-site reference details**: the TestMu AI entry offers video light/dark tabs, desktop light/dark captures and a mobile capture. Its desktop light view rendered during review; its video light preview reported that the media could not play.
- **DESIGN.md export**: site records include an extracted design summary with typography, color palette, layout and style tags, plus a downloadable Markdown file. TestMu's record lists Manrope and Roboto and a mostly grayscale palette.
- **Live source link**: open the original site alongside its gallery capture. The TestMu capture was dated May 2026; the current TestMu homepage has since changed its copy and visual identity, so older captures may describe a previous version.

## Using it with agents

There is no published MCP, API or CLI listed in the gallery. Individual site records offer downloadable DESIGN.md files that can provide a compact starting brief for an agent. Treat the file as a generated snapshot: check its linked live site before using its claims or copying design decisions, and do not treat third-party captures as licensed assets.

## Watch out for

- Captures and extracted design summaries can lag behind live sites. TestMu AI's May 2026 record does not match its current homepage copy and branding.
- Some video previews may not play; the TestMu light video was unavailable during review, although the desktop light capture rendered.
- A tag for a framework or a listed font helps narrow research, but verify it against the live site before depending on it.
- Pricing and reuse terms were not stated at review. The gallery links to third-party work, so treat its screenshots as look-only.

## Reusable ideas

- Offer the same reference as a still capture and a scroll preview so visitors can study both composition and page pacing.
- Keep color, framework, industry and visual style as separate filters; each answers a different design question.
- Put typography, palette and layout notes beside each capture, and label the capture date so people can judge whether the summary is current.
