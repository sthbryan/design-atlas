# Motionimo

## What it is

Motionimo is a curated library of motion design work: showreels, brand films, commercials, explainers, title sequences, kinetic type and logo animations. This is linear motion graphics, not interface code. It claims 500+ picks; the sitemap listed about 450 video pages at review time, plus around 150 resource pages and a small journal. Mathis Fournier designs, develops and maintains it. The about page describes it as a free project for sharing good references, and submissions are free.

## When to open it

When you are planning a launch film, product teaser, animated logo or brand sting and want to see how studios handle pacing, transitions and type in motion. It is less useful for UI micro-interactions; for those, product-focused galleries show more relevant behaviour.

## Most useful

- **Filters** by technique (2D, 3D, mixed, cel), format (showreel, commercial, brand film, short film, explainer, title sequence, kinetic type, teaser), colour and industry
- **Logo animations** as their own section, useful for intro and outro stings
- **Studios directory** for finding who made a piece you like
- **Resources**: 100+ tools, plugins, software and learning materials for motion designers, plus a Cavalry vs After Effects comparison in the journal
- **Collections and private notes** for building a moodboard (Pro)

## Using it with agents

There is little for agents here. The `llms.txt` only lists the site's pages; there is no API, MCP, prompts or code, and robots rules block the saved and collection pages. Use it by hand: pick a reference, note its duration, rhythm, easing and type treatment, and give that description to an agent building the motion in code or in a tool such as Remotion.

## Watch out for

- The free tier is tight (three detail views a day), so browsing seriously means paying
- Pricing on the site and in `llms.txt` disagree, and the about page still calls it a free tool; check the live pricing page before you buy
- The terms were last updated in December 2024 and give a contact on a different domain (motionimo.com)
- The work is linear video craft, not interactive UI; timing lessons carry over, but implementations don't

## Reusable ideas

- Build a launch video around one strong transition that links scenes, instead of many unrelated effects
- Animate the logo as a short sting that can open and close videos and product loaders
- Use kinetic type to carry the message when there is little footage of the product
- Keep a motion moodboard filtered by technique and format, so a brief can name "2D explainer" or "3D teaser" precisely
