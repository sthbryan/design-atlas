# 404s

## What it is

404s is a curated gallery of creatively designed 404 error pages, built on the premise that "404 pages are where joy and error meet." It's a project by Finnian Sturdy.

## When to open it

- When your project's 404 is still the generic framework default and you want to turn it into a memorable brand moment.
- When you need a 404 reference with a specific interaction: animated, custom cursor, mini-game or scroll-driven.
- When you're looking for a specific visual style (glitch, brutalist, editorial, large type) within a specific industry (fintech, e-commerce, media, nonprofit/gov).

## Most useful

- Style filter: 3D, bold, brutalist, colorful, dark, editorial, glitchy, hand-drawn, illustrative, large type, light, minimal, photographic, playful, retro, typographic.
- Interaction filter: animated, custom cursor, game, hover effects, interactive, scroll-driven.
- Industry filter: agency/studio, e-commerce/retail, entertainment/arts, finance/fintech, media/publishing, non-profit/gov, personal/portfolio, tech/SaaS.
- Its own keyboard shortcuts: `g` for grid view, `s` for "surprise me" (random example).
- An explicit legal notice: screenshots are shown only to celebrate design, never to reproduce or redistribute copyrighted material.

## Using it with agents

There is no MCP or export. Its `llms.txt` is a usage policy rather than an index: it asks for credit to 404s.design and the original designer, allows reuse of its code examples with attribution, and discourages direct copying and bulk downloads. Use it visually: open an example matching the style/industry you need and describe its structure and interaction to the agent (e.g. "a 404 with a centered illustration, a subtle hover animation and a back-to-home button") so it implements it.

## Watch out for

- The site itself states all screenshots are © their original owners and shown only for inspiration, never to copy or redistribute.
- Being such a narrow niche, the volume of examples is smaller than in general galleries; pair it with another source for variety.
- No licence or pricing is stated for the site itself.

## Reusable ideas

- Combining three filter axes (style, interaction, industry) in a niche gallery gives enough depth without needing thousands of entries.
- A "surprise me" shortcut (`s`) for random browsing is a simple way to boost exploration time on a small gallery.
- Explicitly stating the copyright policy in the footer removes ambiguity about how the shown content may be used.
