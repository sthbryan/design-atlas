# Paper Animation

## What it is

A generator for one specific motion motif: paper. You upload an image and select an object, or type a word, and it returns a clip in one of four styles — Paper Fold, Newspaper Highlight, Magazine Letters, Press Coverage. The newspaper mode is the most developed: ten or more frames per generation, fifteen newspaper typefaces, justified columns and paper texture and creases, so a keyword animates across what looks like a stack of real clippings rather than a single template with text on top.

## When to open it

Open it when a launch clip or a section header needs a tactile, editorial transition and you do not want to shoot paper by hand. It is also a quick way to see how a single word behaves at different speeds and directions before commissioning the real thing.

## Most useful

- **Four distinct styles**, not four skins: fold, highlight, magazine letters and press coverage differ in composition, not just colour.
- **Per-style controls** for size, distortion, texture and animation direction, which is where most of the character lives.
- **Background removal** on the uploaded object, so a cut-out can sit on its own paper ground.
- **Green-screen mode**, the detail that makes the clip usable inside an existing edit rather than only on its own.
- **MP4 and GIF export** at a fixed size, which covers both video editors and slides.

## Using it with agents

No `llms.txt`, MCP server, API or CLI was found at review, and generation happens in the browser behind an account. An agent cannot produce a clip here. What it can do is specify the motion: which style, how many frames, direction, and where the clip sits in a timeline, once a person has made the asset.

## Watch out for

- The aesthetic is narrow. Paper collage reads as a trend, and it will date quickly in a brand asset.
- Free exports carry a watermark, and the paid tiers are sold with founder-numbering and scarcity language.
- Fifteen newspaper faces are approximations of print type; they do not have the metrics of the originals.
- Generated motion is not editable as a timeline. If the timing is wrong you regenerate, you do not keyframe.
- Several features listed as coming soon, including transparent PNG export, are not available at review.

## Reusable ideas

- Offer a handful of named styles with their own composition rules instead of one canvas and a pile of sliders.
- Export a green-screen variant so the asset can be composited rather than only watched.
- Show the frame count per generation; it tells a designer how much motion they are buying.
- Publish the roadmap next to the pricing, so the limits of the current version are explicit.
