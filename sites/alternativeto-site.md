# AlternativeTo

## What it is

AlternativeTo is a browsable directory of software products and their alternatives. Its homepage includes category pages, comparison counts, featured apps, popular product swaps, and community-submitted listings. It is useful as a discovery layer: find a product in a field, then follow its links to the original product site for visual research.

## When to open it

- When looking for products adjacent to a tool or interface you already know.
- When starting a broader search for examples in a category such as design, UI or analytics.
- When you need to branch from a known product into competitors and then inspect their live sites.

## Most useful

Start with [categories](https://alternativeto.site/browse), [software comparisons](https://alternativeto.site/software/figma), or [curated lists](https://alternativeto.site/lists). For example, the Figma listing exposes comparisons such as Adobe XD; open the linked products themselves to study their interfaces. AlternativeTo's own visual design is the directory and comparison experience; the external product pages are the inspiration sources.

## Using it with agents

The reviewed homepage did not expose an MCP, API, CLI, `llms.txt` or copyable agent prompt. Treat it as a search directory, then open and verify the source product directly. Site listings are community-submitted and the footer says they are reviewed before publication.

## Watch out for

- A listing or comparison is a discovery lead, not proof that a product is active, available or accurately described; verify it on the product's own site.
- The site's `/terms` path returned a not-found page during review, and no reuse license was found on the homepage. Treat its content as look-only.
- Do not infer a product's interface from its directory card; inspect the original site.

## Reusable ideas

- Connect a known product to alternatives, categories and user-submitted additions so research can fan out from one starting point.
- Pair rankings with counts and concise product summaries to show why a result appears.
- Keep the directory's own presentation distinct from the products it indexes.
