# ROOM 1707

## What it is

ROOM 1707 is a branding and digital studio based in Shanghai. Its portfolio presents client projects alongside short descriptions of the studio's strategy, social media and digital product work.

## When to open it

Use it to study typographic studio pages and how a social campaign can be documented as an extended sequence of mobile layouts, photography and campaign marks.

## Most useful

- **[Home](https://www.room1707.com/)**: a huge, tightly set “WE BUILD BRANDS FOR TOMORROW” headline dominates a pale-gray field. The page uses black type, small navigation controls and project imagery as a counterpoint to the spare opening.
- **[Bershka case](https://www.room1707.com/work/bershka)**: an oversized wordmark and project summary lead into a long run of social posts, campaign logos, phone layouts and fashion photography. The varied campaign colors sit within the studio's mostly black, white and gray page frame.
- The case page labels its industry, project type and service before explaining the localization and social strategy, then lets the visual examples carry the rest of the story.

## Using it with agents

No public MCP, API, CLI, skill or agent-readable guide surfaced in the reviewed pages. An agent can use the public Work archive and case pages in a browser. The terms reserve the site's content, so describe the visual structure without copying its project images or text.

## Watch out for

- Project imagery belongs to its creators and clients; ROOM 1707's terms prohibit reproducing or redistributing site material.
- The case page is a social media strategy example, not a general ecommerce interface pattern.
- Motion was not confirmed during this review; the long sequence is useful for studying changes in image and layout rhythm while scrolling.

## Reusable ideas

- Pair a high-impact project name with a short, readable summary and compact metadata.
- Give each campaign image room to read, then vary the sequence with phone mockups, wordmarks and photography.
- Use a neutral portfolio frame to make the client's changing campaign palettes stand out.
