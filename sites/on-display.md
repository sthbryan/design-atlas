# Websites on Display

## What it is

Websites on Display is a searchable archive of website design references. The home page presents a grid of site previews, and its sidebar groups filters by industry, design style, page type, typeface, color, technology, performance and author. Each record includes preview imagery, design tags and technology details, with an "Open website" link to the live source.

## When to open it

Open it when you have a concrete visual or technical direction to search by, such as monochrome styling, a typeface, or a framework, and want to inspect the current site behind a reference.

## Most useful

- Filter records by style, page type, typeface, color, technology or performance; use the industry and author filters when the visual brief is more specific.
- Open a record such as [Marius Uhlig](https://ondisplay.website/site/mariusuhlig) for its preview, credits, palette, tags and performance scores. The record links to the [live portfolio](https://www.mariusuhlig.com/), whose project index uses a restrained, text-led grid beside large editorial photography.
- The compact-grid control and dark-mode toggle let you adjust how much of the archive is visible while browsing.

## Using it with agents

No MCP, API, `llms.txt` or agent-specific interface was visible on the reviewed pages. An agent can use the public filters, read record metadata and follow "Open website" to inspect the live design. Treat preview images as archive captures; verify the linked site before describing its current appearance.

## Watch out for

- Performance figures belong to individual records and may change as the sites change; use them as a prompt to recheck rather than a lasting rating.
- The archive states that featured designs, screenshots and marks remain the creators' intellectual property. No general reuse licence was stated, so use it as a visual reference and check the creator's terms before reusing work.
- Records include technology and visual tags that can be helpful for discovery, but confirm those details on the live site when they matter to implementation.

## Reusable ideas

- Combine visual tags such as style, typeface and color with practical filters such as page type and technology.
- Pair each preview with credits, a palette and an obvious route to the original site, so a reference is easy to inspect and attribute.
- Let people change grid density and theme without interrupting their current search.
