# DesignMe

## What it is

DesignMe is a design and development studio for funded startups. Its portfolio pairs service pages with long-form case studies. The N3XT case study documents brand exploration, product structure, a design system and a product with more than 300 screens at review; N3XT's current public site is also available to inspect live.

## When to open it

- When you want to see how a studio explains the decisions behind one shipped identity and product.
- When you need a live B2B fintech marketing-site reference with a clear, restrained orange brand.
- When you want to study how a single story carries from brand work into a product and launch site.

## Most useful

- **N3XT case study**: [the project write-up](https://www.designme.agency/projects/n3xt) describes the design system, light and dark product themes, and decisions behind the live launch site.
- **Live example**: [N3XT](https://n3xt.io/) opens with a large “Liberating Money” headline, one orange action colour, and an animated dotted world map with curved routes. Further down, a comparison table contrasts N3XT with traditional banks and payment providers.
- **Design rationale**: the case study explains why the work used a warm orange identity, a brand-led hero, short “how it works” steps and separate paths for finance teams and developers.
- **Price transparency**: the FAQ gives starting ranges for studio work rather than a public fixed-price menu.

## Using it with agents

There is no published MCP, API, CLI or agent guide for the portfolio. Open the case study and live N3XT site directly, then describe the hierarchy, palette and motion you want to study.

## Watch out for

- This is a studio portfolio, not a broad searchable gallery; the strongest example in this review is the N3XT case study.
- The agency states monthly retainers of US$6,000–10,000 and focused projects starting at US$10,000 at review.
- Case-study images and the N3XT brand belong to their respective owners. Use them for visual reference; no reuse licence is stated.
- The live N3XT homepage presents a banking product, so treat its financial claims as product copy rather than independent evidence.

## Reusable ideas

- Explain the link from business problem to design decision, then show the live outcome.
- Keep a single signature colour across navigation, actions and illustrations while letting the headline carry most of the hierarchy.
- Use one simple comparison table to make a complicated market position scannable.
- Give distinct audiences separate navigation paths when they need different information.
