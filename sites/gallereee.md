# Gallereee

## What it is

Gallereee is a portfolio gallery with collections for visual styles such as Typographic, Experimental, Minimal and Dark mode, plus filters for professions such as designer, agency and architect. Its records credit a maker, describe the work and link to the featured live site. At review, the typographic collection listed dozens of portfolios, including agencies and individual designers.

## When to open it

Open the [typographic portfolio collection](https://gallereee.com/style/typographic) when you want type-led portfolio examples, then use a record's Live site link to check the current work. The gallery record and the linked portfolio are separate references: the first helps you discover and classify a project; the second shows the design in its published context.

## Most useful

- The [LxL Creative record](https://gallereee.com/websites/lxl-creative) links to the [live LxL site](https://lxlcreative.co.uk/). The current home page pairs oversized uppercase type and restrained navigation with campaign imagery and service links; use it to study how a type-led identity carries through a working agency site.
- The typographic collection also includes portfolios such as [Cosmos Studio](https://gallereee.com/websites/cosmos-studio), [Sun Hung](https://gallereee.com/websites/sun-hung) and [Monolog](https://gallereee.com/websites/monolog). Each record provides a direct route to its live site.
- Follow the style and profession filters to compare different portfolios around a shared visual quality, then verify details such as responsive behavior and motion on the current source site.

## Using it with agents

No MCP, API or `llms.txt` integration was verified during review. Use a browser to browse the collection and open individual records and their Live site links.

## Watch out for

- A style label is a discovery aid, not proof that every page in a portfolio uses the same approach. Inspect the actual site and the page relevant to your work.
- The collection is free to browse, but no reuse licence for the screenshots or featured work was stated on the pages inspected. Study the references; do not copy or redistribute their work without permission.
- Gallery records include template promotions. Treat those separately from the portfolio being featured.

## Reusable ideas

- Filter a broad portfolio collection by both visual style and profession, then link each record to the current source site.
- Keep the maker, short context, style tags and live-site action together so a visitor can move from discovery to visual verification.
