# Frameset

## What it is

Frameset is a visual-search library for filmmakers, built around film and advertising frames. The Search view pairs a text query and image-search control with a dense grid of stills; Featured and Projects provide separate browsing routes. Opening a result shows an individual frame in an overlay. During review, the “Eating Heartache” result opened as a live example.

## When to open it

Use it when a motion or art-direction brief needs a specific kind of shot, palette or composition and you want to move from a loose visual description to concrete film references. It is a film and advertising reference, not a gallery of web interfaces; translate the framing and colour into your own UI context.

## Most useful

- The search field accepts a visual description, while image search offers another way to find related frames.
- The result grid puts the imagery first, with minimal chrome between the query controls and the references.
- Featured and Projects tabs split discovery from saved work without changing the main search layout.
- Selecting a tile opens a larger single-frame view, so a result can be inspected before continuing through the grid.
- The filter control and result-count slider sit alongside the query, keeping refinement close to the images.

## Using it with agents

No public MCP, API, CLI, agent guide or `llms.txt` was found during review. The browser can still search the visible gallery and inspect an individual result; the site displayed a remaining search allowance without requiring sign-in. The terms prohibit scraping or storing a significant portion of the library.

## Watch out for

- The guest view showed 10 remaining searches; Pro is listed at $15.99 per member per month, with a 32% annual discount advertised at review.
- Terms define the service as reference use for creating original work. Do not reuse library frames in a published advertisement, film or other finished media without permission.
- Results are film and advertising imagery rather than interface examples. Use them to study image direction, framing and palette, then adapt those ideas to the interface.
- The Terms of Use were marked effective September 19, 2026; recheck them before relying on usage limits or content rights.

## Reusable ideas

- Put natural-language and image search directly above a visual result grid so discovery starts with the references themselves.
- Open a selected tile in a larger view without losing the surrounding search context.
- Keep discovery, saved projects and filters available as compact controls around the main image field.
- Let imagery remain the dominant visual layer and keep interface labels restrained, especially in research tools.
