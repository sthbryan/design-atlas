# Chronicle

## What it is

Chronicle generates business presentations and then lets you edit them on a freeform canvas, which is the distinction it keeps repeating: the alternative it names is a generator that hands you slides you cannot change. The page is built for a conservative buyer — "credible decks at the speed of AI", decades of experience at McKinsey, BCG and Apple — and the visual argument is brand control: fonts, colours and visual rules set once, applied to every slide, with layouts that cannot drift outside them.

## When to open it

Open it when you are designing a slide template system and need a reference for how brand rules survive a generator, or when a client asks for a deck tool that will not look AI-made. The template wall is also useful: it labels decks by job — proposals, positioning, roadmaps, board updates, client pitches.

## Most useful

- **Brand rules as the product**: fonts, colours and layout constraints are set up front, which is what keeps generated slides on-brand.
- **Templates credited to named studios** (Apple, McKinsey, BCG, IDEO alumni), rather than an unbounded gallery.
- **Job-based template categories** for sales, marketing, product, business, leadership, consultants and agencies.
- **Diagrams, charts and tables** treated as first-class slide content, not pasted images.
- **Export paths**: PPT, PDF, or publish as a website, so the deck can leave the tool.

## Using it with agents

No `llms.txt`, MCP server or API was found at review, and the product is sales-led. Nothing here is reachable by an agent without an account, so treat it as a reference for how to describe brand enforcement in a design system.

## Watch out for

- No published prices; every route ends in "talk to sales".
- Credentials are borrowed from named consultancies and companies, which is authority by association, not a measurable claim.
- Testimonials are short and unattributed to companies.
- The "serious business" framing pushes toward a narrow, consulting look; for editorial or brand work it is the wrong default.
- The page makes no claim about accessibility of the exported files.

## Reusable ideas

- Sell the constraint, not the generation: state that layout and brand rules cap what the tool can produce.
- Credit the designers behind templates by name where they are known.
- Group templates by the meeting they are for, not by visual style.
- Offer a publish-as-website path so the deck has a durable URL.
