# ArtCenter Spring 2025 Grad Show

## What it is

ArtCenter's Spring 2025 Grad Show is a public catalogue of graduating students and their portfolios. Its white interface uses thin dividers, generous spacing, a bold orange dot and orange link accents, with the term and school navigation above the content. The 2025 page listed 327 graduates at review. Student work appears in profile and project galleries; the catalogue's restrained shell is distinct from those projects.

## When to open it

Open it when you are shaping a searchable student showcase or want to compare interaction design work in a portfolio system that keeps department context and individual projects easy to reach.

## Most useful

- **Search filters**: narrow by graduate name, term, department and type of work.
- **Department routes**: explore Interaction Design and other departments before opening a profile.
- **Graduate profiles**: profiles group a student's projects, academic programme, short biography and external links.
- **Project pages**: Colin Feng's *WEBALL* is one verified Spring 2025 Interaction Design example, with a project description and a sequence of images. That work's app screens are student work, not the gallery's visual system.

## Using it with agents

No MCP, API, CLI or agent guide surfaced in the show pages at review. A direct `/llms.txt` check was blocked by the browser, so its availability could not be confirmed. Open the term page and use the filters or department links to select examples; share the project URL and a written description of the pattern to study.

## Watch out for

- This URL is the Spring 2025 archive; the same catalogue now links to newer terms.
- Some individual projects contain student-authored descriptions and images. ArtCenter's terms prohibit reproduction or redistribution without permission.
- The page is a portfolio showcase, not a component library or reusable asset source.

## Reusable ideas

- Let a visitor narrow a large cohort by several useful dimensions, then carry the selected term and department into each profile.
- Keep graduate identity and project tiles visually separate so the profile remains useful even when a project has many images.
- Use one accent colour consistently for navigation state and actions within a quiet, spacious catalogue.
