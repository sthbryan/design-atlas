# David Bagdasaryan

## What it is

David Bagdasaryan's portfolio opens with a full-bleed portrait on white, a small dot-built mark over the face and a sparse row of identity, location and contact details. The visible page links to Framer templates and an X profile rather than presenting a long project archive.

## When to open it

Open it when exploring a personal portfolio that uses one strong image as the whole first impression, with a compact identity block and minimal actions around it.

## Most useful

- **Hero image**: a close portrait fills the viewport and fades into a soft dark foreground, so the subject carries the page without a separate headline block.
- **Personal mark**: a bright dotted wordmark is centered over the portrait as a small signature.
- **Edge details**: name and role sit at top left; live local time and a booking action share a small capsule at top right.
- **Compact footer actions**: template and social links sit in dark rounded controls near the bottom, with copyright and contact details beneath.

## Using it with agents

No published agent channel was found. Share the portfolio and ask an agent to inspect the contrast between the edge-aligned text and centered portrait, then describe how the same hierarchy could adapt to smaller screens.

## Watch out for

- The current home page is a single visual introduction and does not expose case studies or a deeper project archive.
- The large portrait and centered mark depend on their particular image crop; test legibility and focal placement at narrow widths.
- No reuse licence or public design-service pricing was stated at review.

## Reusable ideas

- Let one commissioned image carry a portfolio's opening view, then reserve small text blocks for practical identity and contact details.
- Anchor utility details at the edges while keeping the visual signature near the image's focal point.
- Use one consistent pill shape for unrelated secondary actions to keep a sparse page visually coherent.
- Include a live detail such as local time only when it supports the creator's identity and remains legible.
