# SNASK

## What it is

SNASK is a creative agency based in Stockholm, Sweden. Its home page opens on a dark, full-screen video with a centered white headline, then moves into service details and a long stream of image-led case links.

## When to open it

Open it to study a bold agency introduction built from moving imagery and type, or to see how a studio can carry a strong visual voice through a long portfolio.

## Most useful

- **Home page** — a narrow uppercase navigation runs along the top edge of the video hero, while a large rounded headline and shorter supporting line sit over its center.
- **Case pages** — a [case layout](https://snask.com/case/5man-rebrand-packaging/) places a centered title and short lead above a nearly full-width image sequence; back, next and read-more links are clearly separated.
- **Long-page rhythm** — service summaries, work links and studio information alternate across the home page, so it is useful for studying a studio site with several kinds of content.

## Using it with agents

No MCP, API, CLI or agent guide is published; `/llms.txt` returns the site's not-found page. An agent can browse the site and follow its public case pages.

## Watch out for

- The opening video and centered white type depend on a dark background. Keep core navigation and text legible when the video is paused or unavailable.
- No public service prices or reuse licence were stated at review. Treat the work as look-only.

## Reusable ideas

- Place the main headline in a clear central area of a full-bleed video and keep navigation near the top edge.
- Use distinct back, next and read-more controls to help visitors move through long case pages.
- Alternate case imagery with short text sections to give a long studio page a readable pace.
