# Detail

## What it is

Detail sells an automated bug-finding and repair service for software teams. Its home page uses an unusually graphic black-and-white hero, with a sharp rising edge, a thin red marker and a field of red bars leading into customer evidence. The hero also exposes a canvas labelled “Shave Sort Visualization.”

## When to open it

Open the [home page](https://detail.dev/) when a developer-tool launch page needs a strong chart-like visual without putting a fabricated product dashboard at the centre. It is one live site's design, not a reusable library.

## Most useful

- Read the black field, white diagonal and red bars as one directional graphic: the eye moves from the headline towards the proof below it.
- The short hero copy and two contrasting calls to action keep the message ahead of the custom illustration.
- The [open-source examples](https://detail.dev/oss) show how repository names, scan results and fixed-bug counts can serve as product proof. Pricing lists a free first scan and a Team plan at $30 per committer per month at review.

## Using it with agents

The home page links to [Detail's documentation](https://docs.detail.dev/), but no MCP, CLI, public agent skill or root `llms.txt` was confirmed during this review. Use the live page as a visual reference; any agent access to the service itself requires an account.

## Watch out for

- The chart graphic is specific to software testing. Borrow its directional composition only when it supports the story you are telling.
- The canvas is labelled in the page's accessibility tree, but its motion and reduced-motion behaviour were not fully evaluated here.
- Terms say customers retain ownership of code and generated fixes. That does not grant a licence to reuse Detail's marketing visuals.

## Reusable ideas

- Let a geometric boundary divide the hero and continue it into a related data graphic below, so the visual tells one story across sections.
- Put concrete evidence near a broad product promise, and keep each proof item short enough to scan.
