# Coca-Cola Visual System

## What it is

The Coca-Cola Company’s July 2026 announcement of a refreshed global visual identity, with an embedded 91-second film at review. The page names and shows the core elements intended to unify Coca-Cola executions across packaging, retail, equipment and digital experiences.

## When to open it

Open the [announcement](https://www.coca-colacompany.com/media-center/coca-cola-sharpens-its-identity-under-one-bold-visual-system) to inspect how a long-running beverage identity is being carried into current applications. Treat the film and release as a view into this rollout, not as a complete set of brand guidelines.

## Most useful

- The film presents the brand’s recognizable red and white alongside the Dynamic Ribbon, Arden Square and Spencerian lettering. Watch for how those established assets are given more visual prominence together.
- The release identifies distinct product treatments too: Coca-Cola Zero Sugar uses larger variant lettering, a black ribbon on cans and a black PET cap. This makes the master identity and sub-line distinction concrete.
- The page’s company-news layout sets a large, black sans-serif headline and small release metadata above a broad video panel on a pale background. The visual work itself sits in the embedded film.

No public MCP, API, CLI, skill or agent guide was found. The company’s `/llms.txt` path returned 404 at review. An agent can study the page and embedded film in a browser; the film is not a licensed asset source.

## Watch out for

- This is a short company announcement and film, not a standards document or a full cross-market application archive. Check the official Brand Center for internal brand guidance only if access is available to you.
- Coca-Cola’s terms say the company and its licensors own the site content and marks, prohibit unlicensed reproduction and reserve use rights. Recreate the underlying hierarchy with original assets; do not copy its video, packaging imagery, logo or lettering.

## Reusable ideas

- Carry a small set of distinctive assets through several product and environment formats, while giving variants one clear differentiating cue.
- Put a short visual film beside a concise list of the system’s components so viewers can connect names to applications.
- Use a strong signature device as a bridge between familiar brand elements and newly introduced executions.
