# Rebrand Gallery

## What it is

Rebrand (rebrand.gallery) is a reference library for brand designers, founded in July 2023 by the French studio Sahkyo. It collects new visual identities rather than websites: each rebrand has its reveal video (an "Intro"), a bento board of the identity's pieces, and still "shots" of the system in use. Every entry is tagged by hand with studio, industry, year, style, feel and typeface.

## When to open it

- When you're setting the visual identity that a site or product will sit on, not only the interface.
- When you want to see how one typeface or style (wordmark, geometric, 3D, gradient) holds up across a whole brand system.
- When you need applied examples such as packaging, signage, billboards, merch or brand guidelines.

## Most useful

- About 210 rebrand pages and about 525 bentos in the sitemap; the pricing FAQ counts over 4,000 shots in the full library.
- Shot collections by use: billboard and out-of-home, packaging, brand guidelines, signage and wayfinding, brand motion, merch, editorial and stationery.
- Browse pages for about 120 typefaces, 49 styles, 27 feels and 20 industries.
- Rebrand pages credit the studio (or "In house"), give the year, link a case study where one exists, and offer asset downloads on Pro.

## Using it with agents

No MCP, llms.txt or API, and the terms restrict automated collection and AI-made derivative work, so keep it manual. Study a rebrand, then write down what you learned in your own words (typeface roles, colour behaviour, logo lockups, motion rules) and give that to the agent as a style brief. Don't feed it the assets.

## Watch out for

- It isn't a before-and-after comparison site: entries show the new identity, and the old one rarely appears.
- The site asks for a free account to keep browsing, and asset downloads, the full filter set and unlimited collections need Pro.
- Featured work pays: accepted submissions cost €99 for a permanent place and an X feature.

## Reusable ideas

- Pairing a reveal video with a bento board and applied shots shows a brand as a system, not a logo.
- Tagging by "feel" (vibrant, confident, warm) alongside style and typeface matches how clients describe what they want.
- Filing applications by medium (packaging, signage, merch) makes it easy to check a system beyond the screen.
