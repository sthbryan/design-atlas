# Luzhniki identity — Art. Lebedev Studio

## What it is

Art. Lebedev Studio's case for the renewed identity of Luzhniki, a sports and park complex in Moscow. The studio's own location page identifies Moscow as its main office.

## When to open it

Open the case when a place or multi-use venue needs one identity to cover several activities, facilities and physical touchpoints.

## Most useful

- The [case study](https://www.artlebedev.com/luzhniki/identity/) opens on a saturated blue field with a large white title and a short project brief. The project gallery then shows the identity on posters, apparel, signs and other applications.
- Repeated arcs drawn from the mark become a framing device: they isolate different activities in campaign images and also form an all-over pattern.
- The studio pairs the arc motif with a custom typeface built around similar curves, while facility-specific marks retain the same visual language.
- The [studio home](https://www.artlebedev.com/) introduces its broad portfolio through linked discipline labels; the case is a focused example of its identity and environmental work.

## Watch out for

- This is a brand identity case, not a reusable UI kit. Study how the shapes and type carry across media rather than copying its artwork.
- The case imagery, custom type and identity have no reuse licence stated; keep them as visual references.
- The project concerns a Moscow venue. Art. Lebedev Studio also lists offices in other cities, so the venue's location should not be confused with the studio's only location.

## Reusable ideas

- Derive a campaign frame from the logo so it can focus a wide range of scenes without needing a new graphic device for each one.
- Echo a distinctive mark in a custom typeface to make the system feel related even when the mark is absent.
- Give sub-venues their own identifiers while preserving a shared shape and colour vocabulary.
