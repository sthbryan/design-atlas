# Gil Huybrecht

## What it is

The portfolio of designer and art director Gil Huybrecht. A black canvas holds a compact line of service and recognition details above a large, image-led project grid. The live page lists work such as Studio DADO, The Lookback, DoThings and Vucko.

## When to open it

- When a portfolio should give project imagery most of the page and keep biography and service details brief.
- When studying how a dark gallery can make varied client work feel like one continuous surface.

## Most useful

- **Project grid**: many selected case studies are visible together, with multiple image panels or sequences under each project name.
- **Top index**: services and award recognition sit in narrow columns across the top instead of becoming a long introduction.
- **Project view controls**: switch between a normal grid and a Star Wars view, which changes how the portfolio can be browsed.
- **Profile route**: separates fuller background information from the primary work grid.

## Using it with agents

No MCP, API, CLI, registry, `llms.txt` or agent instructions were linked at review. The portfolio is a visual reference.

## Watch out for

- The page begins with a loading percentage indicator; the review browser showed 0% while project thumbnails were already visible. Do not assume the indicator accurately reports when the work is ready.
- Project imagery and client identities remain their owners' work; the site does not state a reuse licence for them.
- No project pricing or freelance rate card was stated at review.

## Reusable ideas

- Keep profile details in a concise index so visitors can begin with the work immediately.
- Use repeated project labels and image sequences to make a long portfolio scannable without heavy card chrome.
- Offer a second browsing mode when a grid alone does not show the whole archive well.
