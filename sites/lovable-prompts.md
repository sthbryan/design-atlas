# Lovable Prompts

## What it is

A prompt library with one discipline most of its neighbours lack: it publishes what a prompt cost to run. Four builds are measured in Lovable's own credits, read from the balance before and after, with dates — a landing page and email opt-in at 1.8 credits, empty states and skeletons at 9.5, a SaaS MVP with auth at 10.4, a built-in SEO review at 1.1.

The prompts themselves are structured rather than conversational: context, numbered features, the screen states a builder skips unless told (empty, loading, error), a build order, safeguards, and a closing instruction that makes the tool ask clarifying questions before writing code. The Pro versions add a growth channel — organic search, product-led invites, outreach — so the build arrives with a distribution assumption instead of a launch project left for later.

The library is 122 free prompts across eight categories (app builder, UI/UX, backend logic, SaaS, landing page, API integration, CRM, admin dashboard) with 152 in the paid bundle, and a free generator that gives three prompts to a free account.

## When to open it

Open it when you are about to paste a one-line brief into an AI builder and want a prompt that names states and questions instead of describing a vibe, or when you want to compare what things cost to generate before spending a plan's credits.

## Most useful

- **Measured credit costs with dates**, which is the most honest number in this whole category.
- **States written into the prompt** — empty, loading, error — because those are exactly what a generator omits.
- **A closing line that forces questions first**, a small trick that prevents a large rework.
- **Build order inside the prompt**, so the agent does not start with the least important screen.
- **A free generator with a quota**, rather than a wall.
- **Guides with dated prices** for seven builders, which age better than comparisons.

## Using it with agents

Prompts are the channel: the page publishes them in full with copy buttons, so an agent can fetch the text of a public prompt and adapt it. There is no `llms.txt` at review and no API. The bundle's Pro versions are behind a payment, and the generator requires an account after three.

## Watch out for

- The site earns commission on Lovable sign-ups and says so; the "try it" links are affiliate links.
- Four measured runs is a sample, not a benchmark, and credits vary with the plan and the app.
- Growth channels written into a prompt are opinionated (product-led invites, outreach) and may not fit the product.
- The bundle price is one payment but the prompts are tied to one builder's prompting style.
- Generated apps still need review: a prompt that names states does not guarantee the states work.

## Reusable ideas

- Publish what a prompt cost to run, with a date, as part of the prompt.
- Name the states a generator forgets, in the prompt, in order.
- End every build prompt by asking the tool what it needs to know.
- Put the distribution plan in the same prompt as the product.
