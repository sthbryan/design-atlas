# Promo AI Videos

## What it is

Promo AI Videos combines a catalogue of promotional-video examples with a generator that turns a product description into a video. The home page displays a mosaic of clips and a large typographic headline; its showcase exposes filters for motion graphics, 3D, cinematic, anime, illustration and aspect ratio. The site listed 85 videos and 103 styles across its pages at review.

## When to open it

Open the [showcase](https://www.promoaivideos.com/#showcase) when exploring motion treatments for product ads, explainers or short launch videos. Use the examples to compare visual directions, rather than treating the generator as a general website-design resource.

## Most useful

- Filter the showcase by style and aspect ratio, then play an individual example to study pacing and motion in context. The page says clips play with sound.
- The [Styles index](https://www.promoaivideos.com/styles) groups treatments such as kinetic typography, product walkthroughs, mascot explainers, infographics, editorial looks and retro pixel art.
- The [Models page](https://www.promoaivideos.com/models) separates the director model from the video models used to render a result.
- Pricing is based on one-time credit packs rather than a recurring subscription; the Starter pack was $19 at review.

## Using it with agents

No MCP, CLI, public skill or root `llms.txt` was confirmed. The generator is a browser product; an agent can help shortlist style categories, but generating a video requires the site's own workflow. Its terms allow commercial use of videos a customer makes; that permission does not cover videos in the public showcase.

## Watch out for

- Clips can include third-party AI output. The terms warn that results may resemble other work and make the customer responsible for claims, brand assets and images they provide.
- Do not download or reuse gallery videos as assets; no licence for those examples was stated.
- Model availability and prices for new generations can change. Check the current [pricing page](https://www.promoaivideos.com/pricing) before using the generator.

## Reusable ideas

- Put actual examples beside the main proposition so visitors can judge the output before creating an account.
- Let users narrow visual references by medium and aspect ratio, not just by industry or product name.
- Pair a broad style label with a concrete clip title so a gallery stays browsable without flattening different motion approaches into one category.
