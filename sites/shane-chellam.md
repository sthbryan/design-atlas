# Shane Chellam

## What it is

Shane Chellam's portfolio offers two ways to follow a career story: a scenic interactive 3D ride through four stages, or a one-page recruiter view. The opening screen sets that choice against an illustrated landscape with a road along its bottom edge.

## When to open it

Open it for an example of an ambitious portfolio experience that still gives visitors a fast, conventional route to the useful details, or to study a career narrative mapped onto a traversable world.

## Most useful

- **Two entry paths**: the “Take the ride” card describes the four-stop interactive route, while a neighboring “Recruiter view” promises the same information on one page.
- **Landscape as navigation**: a small cyclist and four labeled destinations sit along a winding road, making the route legible before the visitor starts.
- **Scene palette**: blue evening sky, layered green hills and dark translucent cards separate the content from the illustrative world.
- **Quest log**: a compact panel tracks education, work and project goals as progress items, with links to the corresponding details.
- **Clear interaction expectation**: the site names the ride's approximate duration and desktop preference, then offers a music toggle beside the start action.

## Using it with agents

No agent-facing integration was visible during the review. The opening choice, route labels and recruiter-view link were readable in the browser; the full ride, audio and narrow-screen behavior were not tested.

## Watch out for

- The scenic route is described as best on desktop and takes about four minutes according to the site; the one-page view is offered as the faster route.
- The interactive ride was not completed during this review, so later scenes and reduced-motion behavior remain unverified.
- No reuse licence was found for the site, artwork or music. Treat it as look-only.

## Reusable ideas

- Offer an expressive route and a direct route side by side when they serve different visitor needs.
- Let a visual map explain an interactive journey before the visitor commits to starting it.
- State the time and device expectations beside the action so people can choose knowingly.
