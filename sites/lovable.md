# Lovable

## What it is

Lovable is an AI app builder that turns a prompt into a working application. The landing page makes the prompt box the central call to action, so the core interaction is tried on the homepage itself rather than behind a video or a signup wall.

## When to open it

- When an AI builder's homepage must put the prompt first, with examples for visitors who arrive without an idea.
- When the audience includes non-developers, and the page has to prove ease before asking for signup.
- When community creations are the social proof, and the site must show them as live apps rather than screenshots.

## Most useful

- The prompt box as the hero: trying the product is the same action as reading the headline.
- Example prompts and community apps beside the box, so a blank page is not the starting experience.
- The page's structure follows the builder arc (describe, generate, publish) instead of a feature list.
- Live community apps as proof, which is stronger for this audience than testimonial quotes.

## Using it with agents

- No agent channel is declared on the marketing site at review; the platform itself is the agent surface, behind an account.
- The public pages are the agent reference; building projects needs an account.

## Watch out for

- Credit and plan terms at review are stated on the pricing page; confirm live before budgeting, since AI generation is usage-based.
- Community apps on the site are other users' work; do not reuse them.
- All content is proprietary and look-only; study the prompt-first structure, do not copy the layout or copy.

## Reusable ideas

- Put the prompt box in the hero when the prompt is the product, with examples for empty-mind visitors.
- Show community creations as live, interactive proof instead of testimonials.
- Order the landing page along the creation arc (describe, generate, publish) rather than product modules.
