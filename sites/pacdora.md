# Pacdora

## What it is

Pacdora is a packaging mockup and dieline template platform: the largest mockup and dieline library, with an AI packaging design agent. It covers box mockups (1,169 at review), bottle (999), pouch and bag (316), can (277), book (51) and t-shirt (27) mockups, plus dieline templates for each.

## When to open it

Open it when you need a packaging mockup or dieline for a product render, a pitch or a print handoff.

## Most useful

- **Category depth**: packaging types in the hundreds to thousands per category at review.
- **Dielines included**: the flat templates sit next to the 3D mockups, so print handoff is covered.
- **AI packaging agent**: generate packaging designs inside the same platform.

## Using it with agents

An `llms.txt` describes the platform for agents; the mockup generator and AI agent are web tools.

## Watch out for

- Paid plans gate part of the library; confirm the licence before using mockups in commercial packaging.
- Mockups are presentation assets, so check dieline accuracy for print.

## Reusable ideas

- Pair every 3D mockup with its dieline so the asset works for both screen and print.
- Organise by packaging type, since that is how designers search for them.
