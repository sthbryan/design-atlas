# Ashish Gogula

## What it is

Ashish Gogula's portfolio introduces a software and design engineer in a compact text column, then presents small project previews and personal interface widgets on a warm off-white background.

## When to open it

Open it for a restrained layout that adds personality through a few functional objects rather than a large hero image. The current homepage includes a recently played music card, an analog clock and a pixel-art figure above the project list.

## Most useful

- The short introduction and contact action appear before the widgets, keeping the professional purpose clear.
- Three neighboring cards give distinct shapes to music, time and pixel art while sharing rounded corners and a quiet border.
- The music card combines album artwork, track metadata and a clear play button in one compact module.
- Blurred project thumbnails below the widgets create a soft transition into the work section.

## Using it with agents

No published agent channel was found. Share the homepage and ask an agent to inspect the profile-to-widgets-to-projects order and the hierarchy within the music card.

## Watch out for

- The music player and live clock depend on external or changing state; show an understandable fallback when data is unavailable.
- Blurred project previews can obscure the work if their loading or scroll state does not resolve.
- No reuse licence was stated for the portfolio content or project imagery.

## Reusable ideas

- Use a small row of personal widgets to give a portfolio character after a concise professional introduction.
- Preserve a clear primary action inside a media card, with artwork and track details supporting it.
- Use a soft transition between personal content and the work section while keeping project titles legible.
