# Chánh Đại

## What it is

Chánh Đại's design-engineer portfolio combines a personal profile with a substantial public catalogue of components, page blocks, projects, writing, experience and recognition. Its homepage presents this material inside a spare, ruled interface.

## When to open it

Use it to study how a personal portfolio can also function as a browsable component and block catalogue, or when you need a line-art, editorial direction for a technical site.

## Most useful

- The opening illustration uses a large isometric outline, faint construction guides and a handwritten cursor note, making the hero feel like an annotated drawing sheet.
- Thin rules divide the page into a compact centered frame; labels, metadata and figures consistently read like captions in a technical document.
- The avatar and name sit across the hero's lower edge, with the role and social links on neighboring ruled bands.
- Components and blocks are introduced with counts, short names and dedicated routes, while projects expand into descriptions, dates and feature lists.
- The page offers a visible theme control, command search and links to agent-readable `llms.txt` and `index.md` files.

## Using it with agents

The footer exposes an `llms.txt` link and an `index.md` link. Use those published files to navigate the portfolio's sections; inspect the live component or block before describing its appearance or interaction.

## Watch out for

- Handwritten notes, illustration and fine rules are part of this site's personal art direction. Dense linework can compete with content at smaller sizes.
- The long homepage includes advertising and extensive recognition and analytics sections; choose a specific section when using it as a reference.
- The footer states that the source is MIT. That does not grant reuse rights for the site's written material, third-party logos or artwork.

## Reusable ideas

- Carry one illustration style through the hero, section rules and marginal annotations to give a technical portfolio a coherent identity.
- Use consistent caption-like metadata and section labels to make a long page easier to scan.
- Pair component previews with a short description and a stable detail route so the portfolio can serve as both profile and catalogue.
