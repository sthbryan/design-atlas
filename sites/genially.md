# Genially

## What it is

Genially makes interactive content — presentations, infographics, quizzes, escape rooms, microsites — and its template gallery is worth reading as a piece of information architecture. The page offers two independent axes: an output axis (Halloween, Games, Surveys, Quizzes, Presentations, Microlearning, Branching scenarios, Infographics, Interactive images, eCards, Microsites, Brochures, Reports, Video, Resumes) and an audience axis (eLearning, Teaching Resources, Business, Marketing, Students, HR, Higher Education). Template names are specific rather than generic: "Bricks Quiz", "Countdown Timer", "3D Onboarding Manual", "Scratch and Discover", "Password Challenge: Guess the Word".

## When to open it

Open it when you are designing a template gallery and need a way to let two kinds of visitor — someone who knows the format they want, and someone who knows the audience — find the same item. It is also a source of named interaction formats for quizzes and onboarding.

## Most useful

- **Two filter axes** that cross: format and audience, with "most popular" as a third entry point.
- **Verb-led template names** ("Match the Concepts", "Follow the Sequence", "Spot the Differences") that describe the interaction instead of the theme.
- **Format categories that include odd ones**: branching scenarios, interactive images, eCards, resumes.
- **A "New" rail** beside the popular rail, so recency has its own lane.
- **Microlearning and onboarding as first-class categories**, which most template libraries do not separate.

## Using it with agents

No `llms.txt`, MCP server or API was found at review. The gallery is readable as text with names and categories, but the templates themselves are interactive and live behind the editor.

## Watch out for

- The gallery is organised for browsing, not for judging design quality: thumbnails, not previews, and no rating.
- Format and audience labels overlap, so the same template can appear under several headings.
- Holiday and seasonal collections (Halloween at review) crowd the popular rail.
- Prices and plan limits are not on the page read.
- The interactive formats are the product's strength, and they are exactly what a static gallery cannot show.

## Reusable ideas

- Give a template gallery two axes, format and audience, and let either one lead.
- Name templates by their interaction, not their subject.
- Promote microlearning and onboarding to their own categories.
- Keep a separate rail for new items instead of mixing them into popular.
