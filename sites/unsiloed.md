# Unsiloed AI

## What it is

Unsiloed turns PDFs, images and spreadsheets into structured data for AI products. Its home page explains the service with an annotated document illustration and worked examples of parsing, extraction and splitting.

## When to open it

When a technical AI product needs to show how data moves through a pipeline, or when a landing page must explain complex document handling through visual examples.

## Most useful

- [Home](https://www.unsiloed.ai/) — inspect the split hero, isometric document layers, magenta and orange annotation lines, and the parsing and extraction examples below.
- [Pricing](https://www.unsiloed.ai/pricing) — compare the usage-based plan cards and included features.
- [Documentation](https://docs.unsiloed.ai/) — see the public integration details; the docs describe an API and page-based processing.

## Using it with agents

The public docs describe API access. I did not confirm an MCP server, CLI, installable skill or `llms.txt` during this review.

## Watch out for

- The document mockups demonstrate the product story; they are not a downloadable asset or a guarantee of processing results.
- Pricing is based on pages and includes paid Standard and Growth plans; check the live pricing page for current rates.
- The terms reserve rights in the site's illustrations and design, so use the page for visual study only.

## Reusable ideas

- Make a complex parsing pipeline tangible with one layered document illustration and callouts for the visible transformations.
- Use small colored annotations to connect interface labels to regions in the main diagram.
- Pair a polished hero demo with raw-input and structured-output examples further down the page, so the product is legible at more than one level.
