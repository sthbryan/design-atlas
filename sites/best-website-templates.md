# BestWebsiteTemplate.com

## What it is

BestWebsiteTemplate.com is a free discovery directory for Webflow, Framer and Shopify templates. The home page is sparse and typographic; its strongest design feature is the template catalogue, where previews, categories, style tags, price and separate design and development ratings help visitors compare starting points. The Aries Webflow page is one concrete example: its preview pairs a dark typographic intro with a bright, image-led agency page.

## When to open it

Open it to shortlist website templates by platform, category, price, style and feature, or to compare a specific template's visual presentation before visiting the marketplace listing.

## Most useful

- **Homepage catalogue**: hover previews let people scan a template's actual page without opening each listing individually.
- **Aries review**: [the Webflow detail page](https://bestwebsitetemplate.com/templates/webflow/aries) includes a moving preview, links to the original Webflow listing, and separate scores for design and development.
- **Filtering**: platform, category, free/paid, style and features can be combined.
- **Scoring methodology**: the site explains how reviewers assess visual quality separately from responsive behavior, CMS structure and code quality.
- **AI guide**: `/for-ai` describes the directory's fields and URL patterns for language models.

## Using it with agents

The site publishes a structured `/for-ai` guide rather than an `llms.txt`. An agent can use it to understand the template record fields and stable detail-page paths, then open the linked preview and original marketplace listing. Treat the displayed scores as the directory's editorial assessment, not a guarantee about suitability or licence.

## Watch out for

- The directory reviews and links to templates; purchases happen on the source marketplace. At review Aries was listed for $129 on Webflow.
- A directory listing does not grant permission to reuse a template, its images or its copy. Check the template's own licence before implementation.
- Scores describe the site's stated methodology and will not capture every project constraint or stylistic preference.

## Reusable ideas

- Let people compare the work itself through short, looping previews before they commit to opening a detail page.
- Separate visual quality from implementation quality so one score does not disguise a trade-off.
- Make filters and review methodology visible near the catalogue rather than hiding them in documentation.
- On detail pages, link directly to the source where the item can actually be inspected or purchased.
