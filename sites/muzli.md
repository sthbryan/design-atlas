# Muzli

## What it is

Muzli curates visual design work through a browser extension, a searchable feed and public collections. The public collection pages group screenshot-led project shots into specific subjects such as landing pages, dark mode screens, dashboards and typography.

## When to open it

Use it to scan a visual direction or compare concepts for a page type. Start with [landing-page examples](https://muz.li/inspiration/best-designed-landing-pages/) or choose another subject from the [collections index](https://muz.li/inspiration/).

## Most useful

The landing-page collection pairs a centered category introduction with a three-column image grid, then opens each project on Muzli Me. The [VICTORY real-estate concept](https://me.muz.li/saikat-hawlader/real-estate-landing-page-ui-design) shows large editorial type over cinematic architecture imagery, a restrained header and image hotspots. Treat it as a concept shot; the source link leads to Dribbble, not a verified live product. Collections make visual scanning quick, but many posts are promotional mockups rather than shipped interfaces.

## Using it with agents

No MCP, API, CLI, `llms.txt` or agent-specific interface was found at review. The public collection and project pages can be read in a browser; an agent should follow the source link and confirm whether a shown design is a live product before describing its behavior.

## Watch out for

The public landing-page list is a small sample of the extension feed and places prompts to install the extension between groups of examples. Project shots may show proposed concepts rather than implemented pages. Check creator attribution and each linked site's terms before reusing anything. Muzli's own terms reserve rights in its material and forbid copying or redistributing it.

## Reusable ideas

Use the subject collections to gather a small moodboard, then compare the live source sites for layout, interaction and responsive behavior. Separate the visible composition in a shot from the behavior of the finished product, and record the creator and original source with each reference.
