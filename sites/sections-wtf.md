# Sections.wtf

- **URL:** https://sections.wtf
- **Type:** inspiration gallery
- **Topics:** inspiration, landing-pages, cta, footers
- **Pricing / licence:** Not stated (free to browse, no account; weekly newsletter, sponsorship by email); the sections shown belong to the sites they come from
- **Reviewed:** 2026-09-25

## What it is

Sections.wtf is a gallery of single website sections rather than whole sites: one hero, one pricing table, one footer at a time, each captured as a short screen recording. It is built on Framer and listed on Peerlist under the handle "aaali"; the curator's full name isn't given on the site. Picks lean towards small studios, indie apps and design-minded products such as Flighty, Craft, Headroom and REKKI.

## When to open it

- When you're building one specific block of a landing page and want a dozen real takes on it in one view.
- When a static screenshot isn't enough and you need to see the scroll, hover or entrance motion of a section.
- When you want less common blocks that most galleries skip, like roadmaps, team pages, comparison tables or Open Graph images.

## Most useful

- About 280 section shots in the sitemap, and most of them play as looping video on the grid.
- 22 section filters: Menu, Hero, Features, List, Bento, Pricing, CTA, Testimonial, FAQ, Subscribe, Contact, Roadmap, Team, Footer, 404, Words, Comparison, Statistics, Form, Open Graph, Tabs and Unsorted.
- Shot pages link to the source domain and list the other sections captured from the same site, plus more shots of the same type.
- A Shuffle button for random browsing and a Submit link for suggestions.

## Using it with agents

No MCP, llms.txt, API or code. Filter to the section you need, pick two or three shots, and describe each one to the agent in layout terms: grid and column count, what moves and when, where the CTA sits, and how dense the copy is. Have it rebuild the block in your stack with your content, and write the motion as separate steps.

## Watch out for

- The catalogue is small next to whole-site galleries, and some types have only a handful of entries.
- There are no style, industry or colour filters, only section type.
- Recordings show how a section behaves, not how it's built; there's no markup, spacing data or accessibility notes.
- The Framer link on the site is an affiliate link.

## Reusable ideas

- Filing by section type makes every comparison like for like, which is what you want when you're designing one block.
- Capturing sections as video keeps the motion that a screenshot drops.
- "More from this site" on every shot lets you read one brand's full page system section by section.
