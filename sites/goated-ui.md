# GOATED UI

## What it is

GOATED UI is a free, curated gallery of live websites, app icons, interfaces and Open Graph images. Its own interface uses a dark card grid, a Web/OG Images switch and visible industry filters; individual records separate the gallery's screenshot and category labels from a link to the original site. A paid sponsor card can appear among editorial entries.

## When to open it

- When you want a fast visual scan of current SaaS, AI, fintech or developer-tool sites and a direct route to the original.
- When you need social-preview references as well as website references; use the separate [OG Images gallery](https://goatedui.dev/og-images).
- When comparing examples in one industry through category pages such as [Developer Tools](https://goatedui.dev/web-design/developer-tools).

## Most useful

- The [homepage](https://goatedui.dev/) exposes industry filters and a card grid. At review, its first entries included Customer.io, Drift, Close CRM, Intercom and Linear.
- An individual [Linear record](https://goatedui.dev/websites/linear-6333) includes a screenshot and category tags, a related developer-tools group, and a **Visit Website** link to [linear.app](https://linear.app/). The gallery record and Linear's live product are separate references.
- The [OG Images section](https://goatedui.dev/og-images) offers a different reference type: social preview compositions rather than full page layouts.

## Using it with agents

No MCP, API, CLI, `llms.txt` or agent guide was listed. Ask an agent to use the category pages to shortlist a few examples, open the original sites and compare specific decisions such as type scale, section order, imagery and responsive behavior. GOATED UI describes its gallery as inspiration; its terms also warn that featured designs and screenshots belong to their respective creators.

## Watch out for

- The gallery screenshots and marks remain the property of their respective owners. Its terms permit browsing but state no licence for reusing those assets.
- Sponsored placements are labelled; separate them from the editorially curated grid when evaluating examples.
- A screenshot is a point-in-time preview. Follow **Visit Website** and recheck the live page before relying on it.

## Reusable ideas

- Put website and social-card references in separate but adjacent collections, since their composition goals differ.
- Keep industry filters visible and repeat the category context on each detail page so browsing does not lose its thread.
- Place the original-site link beside the screenshot rather than requiring a visitor to search for the source.
