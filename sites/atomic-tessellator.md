# Atomic Tessellator

## What it is

Atomic Tessellator presents materials research aimed at constrained mineral supply chains. Its home page uses a dark, restrained visual system and an point-and-line visualization below the opening statement.

## When to open it

When a scientific or industrial site needs to feel precise and technical without defaulting to a generic dashboard, or when a hero illustration must lead into a service narrative.

## Most useful

- [Home](https://atomictessellator.com/) — inspect the small uppercase navigation, centered serif headline, quiet CTA and green line graph.
- [Capabilities](https://atomictessellator.com/capabilities) — follow the linked explanation of simulation, synthesis and application.
- [New Materials](https://atomictessellator.com/new-materials) — see how the company presents its materials work.

## Using it with agents

The public site is browser-readable. I did not find a public `llms.txt`, API, CLI, MCP server or installable agent skill during this review.

## Watch out for

- The home page's animated plot is brand-specific; it does not establish that the graphic is live scientific data.
- The site links to a Privacy page but provides no public prices or content reuse licence.

## Reusable ideas

- Keep a technical hero focused on one serif statement, a short supporting line and one action.
- Continue the dark canvas into a fine-line visualization that can carry the scroll into the following section.
- Use numbered capability steps and uppercase labels to organize specialized material without adding a conventional dashboard grid.
