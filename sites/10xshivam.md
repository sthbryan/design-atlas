# 10xShivam

## What it is

Shivam's developer portfolio brings together his introduction, work history, projects, blog posts and contact links.

## When to open it

Open it for a dark portfolio that uses visible outlines and expandable rows to organize experience and project details.

## Most useful

The page uses a black background with a narrow, centered content frame marked by dashed vertical edges. Matching dashed rules divide the header and outlined work entries. A high-contrast serif face gives the name and organization titles a distinct voice against sans-serif supporting text. Project rows pair a large capture with a summary and compact technology chips.

## Using it with agents

No agent-facing integration was found. Projects and blog posts are linked from the top navigation.

## Watch out for

The subdued gray supporting text and thin dashed borders need contrast checks against the black background.

## Reusable ideas

- Use dashed outlines consistently for navigation, section boundaries and expandable entries.
- Pair a serif display face with plain sans-serif descriptions to keep a dark portfolio from feeling monolithic.
- Keep project captures large enough to show product context beside the explanation.
