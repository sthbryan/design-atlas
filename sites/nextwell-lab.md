# NextWell Lab

## What it is

NextWell Lab is a personal collection of browser experiments grouped into full-screen scene galleries: 3D rooms and objects, cursor effects, games, clocks, Pomodoro timers and small apps. The landing page links directly to individual scenes and includes controls to disable animation and apply a visual experiment.

## When to open it

Use the [3D Lab](https://nextwell.top/3d/) for isometric rooms and objects, or [Interactive](https://nextwell.top/interactive/) for cursor-reactive effects and visual studies. Each scene has its own route or hash identifier, such as [Summer Cottage](https://nextwell.top/3d/#/cottage), [Liquid Metal](https://nextwell.top/interactive/#/liquid-metal) and [QlockTwo](https://nextwell.top/clocks/#/qlocktwo). The 3D Lab combines a large stage with a pinned scene list, category filters, keyboard navigation and fullscreen/hide-interface controls.

## Most useful

- A visible scene index makes it easy to compare many treatments without losing navigation context.
- Entries identify the implementation medium, including pure CSS3, Three.js, WebGL, Canvas and GSAP.
- Scene-specific URLs make references easy to share and revisit.
- The landing page's motion toggle gives visitors a direct way to stop animations.

## Using it with agents

No published agent channel was found. Link to a specific scene and describe the visible behavior and controls. The technology label is a clue, not a guarantee that a particular technique will fit another project.

## Watch out for

- This is one maker's experiment collection rather than a reviewed cross-site gallery.
- Free browsing does not grant reuse rights; no licence for the creative work was stated.
- Some scenes need WebGL or animation and may not suit low-power devices or reduced-motion users.

## Reusable ideas

- Keep a persistent scene list alongside the active immersive canvas.
- Label demos with the rendering technology to make exploration useful to builders.
- Make motion and interface visibility user-controlled during an interactive showcase.
