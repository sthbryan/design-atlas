# Luma (AI)

## What it is

Luma's creative platform combines its image and video models with third-party models in a workflow for creative teams. The `/app` marketing page uses a black hero with a centered promise, then moves through use-case cards, workflow capabilities, case studies and model options. The examples focus on concrete deliverables such as campaign variations, product imagery, storyboards and localized video.

## When to open it

Open it when designing a landing page for a creative tool or studying how to explain a broad multimodal product through outcomes and workflows. Its current product page is a stronger source for the Creative Agents product than older Dream Machine references.

## Most useful

- **Use-case cards**: each example connects a creative task to a tangible deliverable.
- **Model section**: Luma and third-party models appear in one visual group, making the breadth of the workspace explicit.
- **Workflow explanation**: shared context, collaboration and production export are presented as benefits alongside generation.
- **Pricing**: monthly individual tiers show credits and capabilities; the visible Plus plan starts at $30/month at review.
- **API**: the navigation links to a separate API offering for developers.

## Using it with agents

Luma publishes an API for model access, linked from the product navigation. It also describes “Luma Skills” as a product feature for reusable workflows, but this is not a public installable coding-agent skill. Review the API docs and separate API terms before building an integration.

## Watch out for

- The product and pricing are changing quickly. The $30/month Plus starting price and included credits are the values shown at review.
- The service terms are proprietary; commercial use depends on the subscription, and free-use input/output terms differ from paid use.
- The site showcases generated images and videos. Treat them as examples of product output, not licensed stock assets.

## Reusable ideas

- Describe a creative platform through its finished work, then reveal the capabilities behind it.
- Organize use cases around the deliverable people need rather than around a list of model names.
- Present model breadth as part of one workflow while keeping each model's role legible.
- Make pricing limits and commercial-use scope visible near the plan details.
