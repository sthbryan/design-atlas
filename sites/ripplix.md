# Ripplix

## What it is

Ripplix indexes short UI animation and micro-interaction clips from real products. Browse by app, interaction type or platform; an app page gathers related examples into one place.

## When to open it

- When you need to see a real interaction's timing and state change instead of imagining it from a still image.
- When comparing how a modal, tab, drag action, onboarding step or success state behaves across web, mobile and other platforms.
- When looking for one product's motion language across several screens.

## Most useful

- Open the [Layout interaction page](https://www.ripplix.com/apps/layout-ui-animations). At review it listed nine examples, including modal/overlay, tabs, onboarding, drag-and-drop and rotation. The visible clips show spatial UI in a room-scale AR setting; inspect how the overlay and object movement share the frame.
- Use the platform row on that page to narrow the examples, then open the interaction links such as [Onboarding](https://www.ripplix.com/interaction/onboarding-ui-animations) or [Drag & Drop](https://www.ripplix.com/interaction/drag-drop-ui-animations).
- Product and interaction categories make it possible to browse the same motion problem from two directions.

## Using it with agents

No MCP, API, CLI, `llms.txt` or agent-specific integration was found. The site offers a Figma plugin, but that is a design-workflow extension rather than an agent channel. Share a specific reference URL with an agent and inspect the clip yourself.

## Watch out for

- Ripplix's terms say featured media belongs to the source product owners and is shown for educational and inspirational reference; they prohibit downloading or re-uploading clips. Link to references rather than treating them as production assets.
- The examples reflect captured product states. Check the original product before treating a clip as current behavior.
- Pro is an optional subscription; at review its terms listed advanced search, private collections and an ad-free experience among paid features.

## Reusable ideas

- Index motion examples by both product and interaction so a designer can browse from context or from a UI problem.
- Keep a clip in its app/platform context; the surrounding interface helps explain why the motion exists.
- Show the whole interaction at a glance with a short looping preview, then let the viewer open its specific reference page.
