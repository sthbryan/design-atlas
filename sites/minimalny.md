# Minimalny

## What it is

Minimalny is a curated index of minimal websites. Its home page separates entries into Agency, Portfolio, Design, Development and Resources, with a search field and paginated cards. Each record includes the creator or studio, location, discipline and year; a detail page adds a short description and a link to the live site. The archive showed 529 entries at review.

## When to open it

Open it to browse restrained portfolios and studio sites, especially when you want examples organized by creative discipline or creator location.

## Most useful

- Use the discipline tabs or search field to narrow the index, then follow a record to the original site.
- [Erwan Lhuissier's record](https://minimalny.com/erwan-lhuissier/) links to his [live portfolio](https://erwanlhuissier.com/). The page uses an image-led project index with a small project label and concise navigation; it is a useful example of letting selected work dominate a creative portfolio.
- Each entry includes a location and discipline, which makes the archive practical for browsing beyond visual keywords.

## Using it with agents

No MCP, API, `llms.txt` or agent-specific interface was visible on the reviewed pages. An agent can browse categories, search the index and follow records to inspect current sites. Treat gallery captures as references; verify the live source before describing its present design.

## Watch out for

- The archive is focused on minimal work and should not be treated as a broad survey of web styles.
- Featured screenshots and sites belong to their original owners; no general reuse licence was stated. Check the creator's terms before reusing assets or code.
- A consent panel says Google Analytics runs only after the visitor agrees. No paid browsing tier was shown on the reviewed public pages.

## Reusable ideas

- Make an inspiration index easy to scan with sparse metadata for creator, location, discipline and year.
- Give entries their own detail pages with a clear path from screenshot to current live work.
- Keep category navigation compact and searchable so a focused archive stays quick to browse.
