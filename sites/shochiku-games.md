# Shochiku Games

## What it is

Shochiku Games is a Japanese publisher site with Japanese and English navigation, a rotating feature carousel and a catalog of titles grouped by genre. The REKA page shows the same green frame around an image carousel and a sequence of illustrated information sections.

## When to open it

Use it as a reference for a publisher site that needs to make a growing catalog easy to browse, then provide a reusable structure for individual game pages. The home and [REKA page](https://game.shochiku.co.jp/ja/games/reka/) provide concrete examples of both levels.

## Most useful

- **Homepage carousel**: one large centered promotion sits between cropped neighboring slides, on a green field with circular arrow controls.
- **Catalog**: title cards are grouped beneath a clear GAMES heading and show genre labels before visitors open a title page.
- **REKA title page**: a green, thinly outlined panel frames the title, developer line and a wide media carousel; numbered slide controls sit directly below.
- **Shared frame**: language, menu and publisher links keep consistent positions across the catalog and title page.

## Using it with agents

No published agent channel was found. Share the homepage and [REKA page](https://game.shochiku.co.jp/ja/games/reka/) and ask an agent to map how the shared publisher frame separates catalog navigation from a title's media and product details. Treat the game imagery and marks as look-only.

## Watch out for

- The catalog depends on cover art to distinguish titles; keep genre and title text available independently of each image.
- The reviewed pages are Japanese-first, with a visible English language route. Check the translated page before using it as an English copy reference.
- Game purchase or availability details vary by title. The publisher site itself is free to browse.

## Reusable ideas

- Put a clear genre tag beside each catalog title so visitors can narrow the collection without opening every page.
- Use a large feature carousel for current releases while keeping the complete catalog one section below.
- Reuse one restrained publisher frame and let title-specific art define the individual pages.
- Provide direct slide controls for visitors who do not want to wait for a carousel to advance.
