# BlueCanvas

## What it is

BlueCanvas is a digital studio that identifies itself as an independent studio in Seoul. Its English home page combines a showreel and interactive graphic studies with client work, design notes and service information. The portfolio names Korean public, media, healthcare and commercial projects, including Forbes Korea, HLB Therapeutics and Seoul Winter Festa.

## When to open it

- When you want to study a studio site that explains interaction through working examples rather than static mockups.
- When a landing page needs cinematic motion, a controllable showreel and a responsive path into case studies.
- When you want to inspect how a Korean studio presents work across enterprise, media and public-sector clients.

## Most useful

- On the [English home page](https://www.specialog.co.kr/en/), the showreel has a visible pause control. Further down, the Live Experience Lab describes a graphic that changes every six seconds and offers Orbit, Sphere and Wave choices; the [interactive-web page](https://www.specialog.co.kr/interactive.php) explains scroll-linked scenes, pointer response and mobile adjustments.
- Follow [selected work](https://www.specialog.co.kr/en/web.php) to open named client examples. The web-service page also lets visitors switch a live demonstration between desktop and mobile and adjust its width.
- Read a case page such as [Website Maintenance and Security](https://specialog.co.kr/en/casestudio/website-maintenance-security.php) when you want to see how the studio pairs a practical problem with explanatory editorial structure.

## Using it with agents

No MCP, API, `llms.txt` or agent export was visible at review. An agent can browse the English pages and follow their case and client links. The studio offers an estimate form and contact channels, but no public design-data export was shown.

## Watch out for

- The site also sells web, marketing, AI and operational services. Its visual portfolio is useful independently of those offerings; estimate ranges on the form are service pricing, not access fees.
- Motion and pointer effects can be harder to inspect without a pointer-capable browser. The studio provides a pause control for the showreel and describes mobile and reduced-motion considerations on its interactive-web page.
- No reuse licence for the studio's design or client assets was stated. Treat the work as look-only.

## Reusable ideas

- Put a clear pause control next to an autoplaying studio reel.
- Make a motion study selectable and explain the interaction directly beside it.
- Pair the full desktop interaction with a live width control so visitors can see how its composition responds to a narrow viewport.
