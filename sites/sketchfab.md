# Sketchfab

## What it is

Sketchfab is a gallery of user-uploaded 3D models presented in an interactive browser viewer. Its model pages support orbiting, zooming and annotations, so a still thumbnail can be inspected from multiple angles.

## When to open it

Use the [3D models gallery](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models) to study object form, surface treatment, lighting and viewer presentation. During review, the model titled “1321” loaded and could be rotated, showing a pale sculptural form against a warm, softly blurred environment.

## Most useful

- **Orbitable preview** turns each model into a small interactive study; the tested “1321” model shows a pale sculptural form against a warm, softly blurred environment.
- **Search and filters** help narrow the catalogue by model category, style and availability.
- **Annotations** can attach labels to points in a 3D scene, useful for product details and explanatory models.

## Using it with agents

The [developer documentation](https://sketchfab.com/developers) lists OAuth, Data, Download, Viewer and oEmbed APIs. Access and capabilities vary by endpoint and account plan. The Viewer API can control an embedded model; downloads require authentication and the model's own licence must be followed.

## Watch out for

- Model performance varies with device and scene complexity; one model encountered during review was too heavy to render, while the lighter “1321” example loaded successfully.
- A model's preview does not grant reuse rights. Check its individual Creative Commons or Sketchfab licence before downloading or adapting it.
- The viewer, Sketchfab branding and platform material remain proprietary; the platform's terms prohibit scraping and reuse of its content outside permitted routes.

## Reusable ideas

- Let people rotate a product or illustration instead of relying on a single fixed angle.
- Use annotations to direct attention to meaningful parts of a complex model.
- Choose an environment and background color that separate the model silhouette from its surroundings.
