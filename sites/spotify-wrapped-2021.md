# Spotify Wrapped 2021

## What it is

An official 2021 interview with Spotify’s global head of brand design about that year’s Wrapped visual identity. It is a historical campaign record, including an image of the 2021 system and an explanation of how the visual direction was built for digital placements.

## When to open it

Open the [2021 Wrapped design interview](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2021-12-01/global-head-of-brand-design-rasmus-wangelin-explains-the-creative-behind-spotify-2021-wrapped/) when studying a single edition of a recurring annual campaign. Keep it distinct from both current Spotify product design and later Wrapped identities.

## Most useful

- The 2021 system centers on a flexible ribbon or thread that can wrap around photography and hold words, year markers, insights and symbols. In the example image, the ribbon’s oversized curved lettering makes a frame around a portrait.
- Standard symbols such as a heart or flame sit beside symbols intended for local markets. That gives one campaign device room to adapt without making every placement identical.
- The interview describes a digital-first campaign where motion is a key part of the design and where the identity needs to work across social, in-app and other digital touchpoints. Use the article’s stills to study the motif; consult the linked 2021 experience for its historical animated behavior.
- Spotify’s black newsroom header and white article body are the publication shell. The ribbon artwork is the campaign identity to inspect.

## Watch out for

- The article explains the 2021 edition only. Wrapped develops a new art direction each year, so do not present this ribbon as the current system or a reusable Spotify template.
- The article is an interview rather than production documentation; it does not provide animation timings, source files or a formal toolkit.
- Spotify’s terms reserve rights to its editorial content and campaign media. Do not reuse the artwork, marks, photos or animation without permission.

## Reusable ideas

- Use one flexible graphic device to connect personalized data, portrait photography and campaign typography across many formats.
- Leave room for local variations within the same visual grammar, so familiar structure can carry details meaningful to a particular audience.
- Plan identity work for both motion and still frames, then check that each remains recognizable on its own.
