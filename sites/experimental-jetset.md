# Experimental Jetset

## What it is

Experimental Jetset is an independent graphic design studio based in Amsterdam, Netherlands. Its site works as an online archive: visitors can search or filter projects by category and change between alphabetical and chronological order.

## When to open it

Open it when you are shaping a searchable design archive, want to study a dense text-based index, or need an example of a project record that keeps its metadata and images distinct.

## Most useful

- **Archive** — project titles and dates form a compact multi-column directory, with search, a category selector and two sorting options at the top.
- **Project records** — a [recent archive entry](https://www.jetset.nl/archive/circuits) begins with a brief text block and navigation to adjacent records, then presents its images in an uneven two-column gallery.
- **Category filter** — categories span print, identity, installation, animation and web work, making the archive easier to browse by medium than by date alone.

## Using it with agents

No MCP, API, CLI or agent guide is published; `/llms.txt` returns a not-found page. An agent can use the archive's search, category selector, sort links and project records in a browser.

## Watch out for

- The homepage prioritizes a large archive over a prominent introduction, so new visitors may need the category selector or search to find a relevant example.
- No public service prices or reuse licence were stated at review. Treat the work as look-only.

## Reusable ideas

- Pair a large archive with both category filters and alphabetical or chronological sorting.
- Keep each record's date and short context close to its title, then let the images occupy their own gallery.
- Provide previous and next links so visitors can continue through adjacent archive entries.
