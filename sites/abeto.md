# Abeto

## What it is

Abeto is a creative studio portfolio for interactive realtime experiences. Its opening composition uses a softly textured botanical illustration as the backdrop to a centered studio statement and a single down-page control.

## When to open it

Open it when you want a studio identity to feel tactile and handcrafted before introducing realtime or 3D work, or when a landing page needs to make one clear invitation into a larger visual experience.

## Most useful

- **Illustrated hero**: a large ochre flower sits behind bold white type, with loose leaves and sketch-like marks spread across a pale yellow field.
- **Contained canvas**: a thin hand-drawn rectangular frame and irregular paper edges give the otherwise full-screen composition a page-like boundary.
- **Focused hierarchy**: the short statement is stacked over the central illustration, with a small circular down control as the only prominent action.
- **Warm palette**: cream, muted olive and burnt orange keep the image natural while preserving contrast for the white headline.

## Using it with agents

No agent-facing integration was visible during the review. The homepage exposed no readable navigation in the accessibility tree, so ask an agent to inspect the live visual directly; deeper interactions and responsive behavior were not verified.

## Watch out for

- The homepage showed a centered loading spinner on a later revisit; its full page and interactions could not be checked consistently. The first loaded view is the basis for the visual notes above.
- No reuse licence was found. Treat the artwork, code and design as look-only.
- Pricing and terms for studio services were not stated on the reviewed opening view.

## Reusable ideas

- Let one oversized illustration carry a studio's identity while keeping the first action visually quiet.
- Add a light drawn frame and paper-like texture to give a full-bleed digital canvas a crafted edge.
- Use a compact, centered headline that stays legible over a detailed image through contrast and careful placement.
