# HK Grotesk Wide

## What it is

HK Grotesk Wide is a display sans from Hanken Design Co. At review, it had seven styles. Its product page pairs green poster and cover layouts with a live FontVisual preview where the same sample can be compared across the family's weights.

## When to open it

Open the [product specimen](https://hanken.co/products/hk-grotesk-wide) for wide display type treatments in posters, headlines and covers, or to study a product page that places finished use examples beside a working font preview.

## Most useful

- **Art direction** — large green and cream panels use black, very wide lettering, outlined corner marks and data-like number cards. The examples show the intended display role more clearly than a plain alphabet sample.
- **Editable comparison** — the embedded tester accepts replacement text, has a size slider and rendered the sample in seven styles at review, from Light through Black.
- **Product structure** — a three-image gallery sits beside the family name, type classification, licence variant and short use description; the preview follows immediately below.
- A linked Bēhance project offers another route into the typeface's visual examples.

## Using it with agents

There is no published MCP, API, CLI or agent guide. An agent can inspect the product page and edit the embedded tester in a browser. Hanken publishes separate desktop, webfont and app licence guides; at review this product was listed at $0.00, while its personal-use terms exclude commercial projects and website or app use without an additional licence.

## Watch out for

- The $0.00 listing is not an open licence. Hanken's personal-use agreement limits use to personal projects, client proposals and trials, and requires an additional licence for commercial use and web embedding.
- Treat the poster gallery as a reference for display typography and layout, not as reusable artwork.
- The sample embed depends on FontVisual, so its availability and controls can differ from the surrounding product page.

## Reusable ideas

- Put finished compositions next to the purchase decision, so the intended use is legible before someone opens a type tester.
- Present the same short phrase in every available style to make family breadth easy to compare.
- Use a distinctive color field, oversized numerals and small utility labels to give a type specimen its own art direction.
