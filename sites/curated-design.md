# Curated

- **URL:** https://curated.design
- **Type:** inspiration gallery · section library
- **Topics:** inspiration, landing-pages, typography-and-styles
- **Pricing / licence:** Free to browse the site gallery; Pro $9/month (7-day trial) for the full section library, advanced filters and CSV/Notion exports. The terms let you study and take ideas from the library but forbid redistributing, republishing or bulk-scraping it; sites shown belong to their owners
- **Reviewed:** 2026-09-25

## What it is

Curated is a gallery of live websites, each reviewed by a person and filed by industry and by visual style, with every entry linking out to the real page. It is run by the team behind [Craftwork](craftwork.md). According to its changelog it opened on Webflow in 2022, spent about eleven months as a section of craftwork.design, and returned to its own domain in August 2026 as an Astro site. Alongside the site gallery it keeps a section library: landing pages cut into heroes, pricing tables, footers and FAQs.

## When to open it

- When you know the look you want (minimal, dark, pastel, neobrutalist) and need live examples in that style.
- When you want to compare many versions of one section type, and a Pro trial is acceptable.
- When you want references you can open at full size, since every entry is a working site.

## Most useful

- About 2,240 site pages in the sitemap, across 16 industries and seven styles (Animated about 1,090, Minimal about 700, Colorful, Dark, Pastel, Gradients, Neobrutalism).
- A section library of 2,267 entries split by type: Features, Hero, Footer, Works, Reviews, FAQ, Catalog, Bento, Statistics, Contacts, Articles, Team, Newsletter and Social proof. 16 are free; the rest need Pro.
- A media filter for animated or static sites, and date and "most saved" sorting (Pro).
- Themed collections, an RSS feed, a weekly email and ⌘K search across sites, sections and templates.

## Using it with agents

No MCP, llms.txt or API. Filter by style and industry, open two or three live sites, and describe the section order, type scale, spacing and colour to the agent. Pro's CSV export can give you a reference list to keep with a project brief, but the terms say the library itself mustn't be republished.

## Watch out for

- Most of the section library is paywalled, and a Pro account is for one person.
- The page also sells Framer templates from Craftwork's store, a paid "skip the queue" review ($29) and $149-a-week sponsor cards, so check what's editorial and what's paid.
- Submissions go through Craftwork's review queue, so the catalogue reflects one studio's taste.

## Reusable ideas

- Two independent axes, what a site is for and what it looks like, let people find "dark fintech" or "pastel portfolio" without a single tag cloud.
- Taking pages apart into sections and lining up forty of one type shows the conventions an industry has settled on.
- Charging for review speed and feedback, not for acceptance, keeps a paid submission path honest.
