# CTA Gallery

## What it is

CTA.gallery is a curated gallery of calls-to-action: buttons, forms, modals and other conversion-focused elements pulled from real sites.

## When to open it

- When you need to improve the conversion of a button, form or modal and want real solutions by CTA type.
- When you're looking for design and copy ideas for a newsletter, pricing/subscription or direct-purchase CTA.
- When you want inspiration filtered by industry instead of just by element type.

## Most useful

- Categories: Button, Call-to-Buy, Download, Form, Modal/Pop-up, Navigation, Newsletter, Pricing/Subscription.
- Additional filter by industry and by mode (light/dark, via the site's dropdowns).
- Examples from brands and studios like Twohands, Wastetide and Webflow, among others.
- A CTA-copywriting tips resource alongside the visual gallery.
- Free downloadable templates from sponsors (Webestica: Framer, Webflow, Bootstrap).

## Using it with agents

No export or MCP; use it descriptively: show the agent the reference CTA (copy, visual hierarchy, micro-interaction) and ask it to adapt the pattern — not the literal copy — to your brand.

## Watch out for

- Mixes sponsored third-party templates with the organic gallery; check the licence on those templates before using them commercially.
- The copy shown belongs to the original brand: use it as a tone reference, don't copy it verbatim.
- No general licence is stated for how the site's own screenshots may be used.

## Reusable ideas

- Classifying CTAs by business function (purchase, download, newsletter, subscription) instead of only by visual shape helps find the right pattern for the actual goal.
- Pairing the gallery with a "how to write it" resource (not just how it looks) adds copywriting judgment to the visual reference.
- Offering free sponsor templates as a hook gives the community an extra incentive to visit the gallery.
