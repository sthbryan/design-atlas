# Godly

## What it is

Godly is a dark, curated gallery of website and app work, plus individual assets such as icons, logos, open-graph images and page sections. The home feed mixes full website captures with app screens and motion posts in a multi-column masonry layout. An entry has a Godly record and may link to a live site; inspect the latter for the current design.

## When to open it

- When you want a visual sweep across landing pages, app screens, logos or a particular section such as a hero, CTA or footer.
- When you want polished, visually assertive product marketing examples rather than a broad directory organized by industry.

## Most useful

- Start at [Websites](https://godly.design/websites) for full-page references, or use the direct [Hero](https://godly.design/hero), [CTA](https://godly.design/cta) and [Footer](https://godly.design/footer) collections when you already know which part you are designing.
- The [GitNimble record](https://godly.design/website/gitnimble) links to the [live GitNimble site](https://gitnimble.com/), inspected at review. Its dark hero centers a compact headline and product description while thin colored paths frame the product capture; study the visual framing rather than its Git-client content.
- The home feed's masonry cards mix live website screenshots with app screens and short motion posts. Choose the media type that matches the surface you are building before browsing further.

## Using it with agents

No MCP, API, `llms.txt` or agent export was visible on the reviewed public pages. An agent can browse the galleries, follow a record to its live site and report composition, typography, palette and motion as separate observations. Do not treat a gallery screenshot as evidence of the featured site's current state.

## Watch out for

- The home feed includes social posts and individual visual assets alongside full websites; a card may not lead to a complete site.
- The gallery does not state a general reuse licence for captures or featured work. Use it for reference and check each creator's terms before reusing anything.
- The public pages reviewed did not show a paid plan or a pricing page; the gallery was browsable without signing in.

## Reusable ideas

- Give high-demand page sections their own direct collections so a designer can jump to a hero, CTA or footer without broad search.
- Label each card by media type and link the full-site capture to both its record and the current live source.
- Use a dark shell and restrained card chrome to let varied screenshots carry the color and visual character.
