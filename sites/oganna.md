# OGANNA®

## What it is

OGANNA is a Taipei-based design studio whose site gathers branding, graphic design, illustration, web, video and animation work. The portfolio separates projects by discipline and includes English, Traditional Chinese and Japanese navigation.

## When to open it

Open it when a brief needs examples of an established studio presenting a broad mix of client work, or when you want to compare identity, editorial, illustration and web projects from one archive.

## Most useful

- **[Home](https://www.oganna.com/)**: a restrained black-on-warm-white frame sets a large, multi-line service statement above a featured-image carousel. A long client-mark strip and a selected-work section follow.
- **[Work archive](https://www.oganna.com/work.html)**: browse by the studio's discipline labels, then open a project for its title, category and images.
- **[2023 Fitness Factory Zhongli Branch](https://www.oganna.com/work-detail.php?id=other-15)**: the case opens with a very large black sans-serif title, small terracotta category marker and a single-line bilingual project label. The page exposes the project image below the heading; the visible case is filed under “Other,” so use other project categories to find identity or interface examples.

## Using it with agents

No public MCP, API, CLI, skill or agent-readable guide surfaced in the reviewed pages. An agent can browse the work archive and follow its discipline links in a normal browser. Use the project pages as visual references and keep their images and client marks as look-only material.

## Watch out for

- The featured project above is categorized as “Other,” so it is not a representative interface example. Follow the archive's Website or Brand categories for those disciplines.
- The footer states that all rights are reserved. Check with the studio or client before reusing any project image, mark or other artwork.
- The work pages identify the discipline and project but provide little written process, so they are stronger for visual inspection than for learning the rationale behind a system.
- Motion was not confirmed during this review; the homepage does expose previous/next controls for its featured work.

## Reusable ideas

- Separate a multidisciplinary archive with visible discipline labels so visitors can move directly to relevant work.
- Let a small category accent and oversized project title establish context before the case imagery begins.
- Keep language switching available alongside the main navigation when the studio serves audiences in several languages.
